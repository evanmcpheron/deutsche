# gerade

**Type:** Adverb; also an adjective ("straight")
**Level:** A1–A2
**Priority:** Tier 2
**Knowledge goal:** Recognition First

[← erst](erst.md) | [Category Index](README.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

## Core Idea

*gerade* pins something to **this exact moment** — right now, just then, at precisely this point.

German has no separate "-ing" tense, so *gerade* is how speakers say **"is currently doing"**:

> **Ich esse.** — I eat / I'm eating.
> **Ich esse gerade.** — I'm eating *right now*.

In casual speech you'll also hear it shortened to **grade**.

## Most Important A1 Uses

### 1. "Right now" — an action in progress

**Ich arbeite gerade.**
I'm working right now.

**Er telefoniert gerade.**
He's on the phone at the moment.

**Wir essen gerade.**
We're in the middle of eating.

### 2. "Just" — a moment ago

**Sie ist gerade gegangen.**
She just left.

**Der Zug ist gerade abgefahren.**
The train just departed.

### 3. "Straight" — the adjective

**Gehen Sie geradeaus.**
Go straight ahead. *(written as one word: **geradeaus**)*

**Der Weg ist gerade.**
The path is straight.

Directions in German lean heavily on **geradeaus**, so this use is worth knowing before a trip.

## Common Chunks

- **gerade jetzt** — right at this moment *(emphatic)*
- **geradeaus** — straight ahead
- **gerade erst** — only just: *Ich bin gerade erst angekommen.* — I've only just arrived.
- **nicht gerade** — not exactly: *Das ist nicht gerade billig.* — That's not exactly cheap.

## Pattern Recognition

**Was machst du gerade?** — What are you doing right now?
**Ich koche gerade.** — I'm cooking at the moment.
**Er ist gerade nicht da.** — He's not here just now.
**Sie hat gerade angerufen.** — She just called.
**Immer geradeaus.** — Straight on.

## What Changes Without It?

**Ich arbeite.**
I work. / I'm working. *(could be a job description)*

**Ich arbeite gerade.**
I'm working right now. *(so this isn't a good moment)*

The difference matters on the phone: *Ich arbeite* tells someone what you do for a living; *Ich arbeite gerade* tells them to call back.

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- **Trying to build a continuous tense.** There's no German equivalent of "am -ing." *Ich bin arbeiten* is not a sentence. Use plain present + *gerade*.
- **Confusing it with *jetzt*.** *jetzt* = now, generally, and can mean "from now on." *gerade* = at this exact moment, often mid-action. See [gerade vs. jetzt](../comparisons/gerade-vs-jetzt.md).
- **Missing *geradeaus* in directions.** If you're lost and someone says *immer geradeaus*, they're telling you to keep going straight, not to hurry.

## Active or Passive?

**Recognition First**, moving to Active fairly quickly — *Ich arbeite gerade* and *Was machst du gerade?* are extremely useful on the phone and in messages, and both are easy to build.

## You May Hear

- **Was machst du gerade?** — What are you up to right now?
- **Er ist gerade nicht am Platz.** — He's not at his desk at the moment.
- **Ich bin gerade unterwegs.** — I'm on my way / out and about right now.
- **Immer geradeaus, dann links.** — Straight on, then left.
