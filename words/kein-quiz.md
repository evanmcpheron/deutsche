## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich habe ___ Geld.*\
   a. nicht\
   b. kein\
   c. keine

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich habe kein Geld.* (*Geld* is neuter, so no ending.)

</details>

2. What situation does **Ich habe keine Ahnung** describe?\
   a. The speaker doesn't know.\
   b. The speaker disagrees.\
   c. The speaker is busy.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** the speaker doesn't know.

</details>

3. Negate this sentence: *Er hat einen Bruder.*

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Er hat keinen Bruder.**

**Explanation:** Masculine accusative → *keinen*.

</details>

4. Which is right, and why? *Das ist nicht Problem* / *Das ist kein Problem*

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Das ist kein Problem.** *Problem* is a noun, and nouns take *kein*, not *nicht*.

**Explanation:** *kein* negates a noun and takes the same kinds of endings as *ein*.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I don't drink coffee" in German, using *kein*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich trinke keinen Kaffee.**

**Explanation:** *kein* negates a noun and takes the same kinds of endings as *ein*.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "We don't have any children" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Wir haben keine Kinder.**

**Explanation:** *kein* negates a noun and takes the same kinds of endings as *ein*.

</details>
