*eigentlich* could sit in either `words/` or `particles/`. Under this repository's placement rule —

> **If a word's main A1-relevant use is as a modal particle — shaping tone or attitude rather than contributing core meaning — its canonical page lives in `particles/`. Otherwise it lives in `words/`.**

— *eigentlich* belongs in `particles/`. In conversation it mostly signals a shift of topic or a mild "come to think of it," which is attitude rather than content.
