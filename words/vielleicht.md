# vielleicht

**Type:** Adverb; also a politeness softener
**Level:** A1
**Priority:** Tier 1 — high
**Knowledge goal:** Active

## Core Idea

*vielleicht* = **maybe, perhaps.** It marks something as uncertain — and, just as often in real speech, it **softens** a suggestion or request so it doesn't sound like an order.

That second job is the one learners miss. *Vielleicht* in a suggestion isn't really about probability; it's about politeness.

## Most Important A1 Uses

### 1. "Maybe" — genuine uncertainty

**Vielleicht kommt er später.**
Maybe he'll come later.

**Ich gehe vielleicht ins Kino.**
I might go to the cinema.

**Vielleicht.**
Maybe. *(a complete answer)*

Note the word order in the first example: when *vielleicht* starts the sentence, the verb comes straight after it — *Vielleicht **kommt** er später*, not *Vielleicht er kommt*.

### 2. Softening a suggestion

**Vielleicht können wir morgen telefonieren?**
Maybe we could talk tomorrow?

**Vielleicht nehmen wir ein Taxi.**
Maybe we should take a taxi.

Here you're not unsure. You're being polite.

### 3. Softening a request

**Haben Sie vielleicht einen Stift?**
Would you happen to have a pen?

**Können Sie mir vielleicht helfen?**
Could you possibly help me?

This is very common when approaching strangers, and it makes the request noticeably gentler.

## Common Chunks

- **vielleicht später** — maybe later
- **vielleicht morgen** — maybe tomorrow
- **vielleicht nicht** — maybe not
- **Haben Sie vielleicht …?** — Would you happen to have …?

## Pattern Recognition

**Vielleicht regnet es morgen.**
**Vielleicht ist er zu Hause.**
**Vielleicht gehen wir ins Café.**
**Hast du vielleicht Zeit?**
**Vielleicht später, ja?**

Notice: sentence-initial *vielleicht* is followed immediately by the verb, every time.

## What Changes Without It?

**Können Sie mir helfen?**
Can you help me?

**Können Sie mir vielleicht helfen?**
Could you possibly help me?

The first is fine. The second is gentler and slightly more tentative — useful with strangers, officials, and shop staff.

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- **Word order.** *Vielleicht er kommt später* is wrong. German keeps the verb in second position: **Vielleicht kommt er später.**
- **Reading every *vielleicht* as real uncertainty.** In requests it's often pure politeness.
- **Confusing it with *wahrscheinlich*.** *vielleicht* = maybe (50/50 or less). *wahrscheinlich* = probably (more likely than not). Recognize the second, use the first.
- **Confusing it with *viel*.** Unrelated words that happen to start the same way.

## Active or Passive?

**Active.** Both the "maybe" use and the polite-request use are worth producing early — *Haben Sie vielleicht …?* is a genuinely useful travel sentence.

## You May Hear

- **Vielleicht später.** — Maybe later.
- **Haben Sie vielleicht einen Tisch für zwei?** — Do you happen to have a table for two?
- **Vielleicht hast du recht.** — Maybe you're right.
- **Na vielleicht.** — Well, maybe.
