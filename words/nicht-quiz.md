## Mini Review


1. Which sentence is correct?\
   a. Ich nicht verstehe das.\
   b. Ich verstehe das nicht.\
   c. Ich verstehe nicht das.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich verstehe das nicht.* German puts *nicht* after the object here, not before the verb.

</details>

2. What does **Er kommt heute nicht** describe?\
   a. He's coming, but late.\
   b. He isn't coming today.\
   c. He came yesterday.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** He isn't coming today.

</details>

3. Fill in the natural negation: *Das Zimmer ist ___ groß.* (not big)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **nicht**

**Explanation:** *Das Zimmer ist nicht groß.* (*nicht* goes directly before the adjective.)

</details>

4. Why is **Ich habe nicht Zeit** wrong, and what should it be?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *Zeit* is a noun, so it takes *kein*, not *nicht*: **Ich habe keine Zeit.**

**Explanation:** *nicht* is used to negate verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and other non-noun elements; its position follows the sentence pattern practiced in the lesson.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I can't come today" in German, using *nicht*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Explanation:** *nicht* is used to negate verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and other non-noun elements; its position follows the sentence pattern practiced in the lesson.

</details>
