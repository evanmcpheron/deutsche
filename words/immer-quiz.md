## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich stehe ___ um sechs Uhr auf.* (always)\
   a. immer\
   b. noch\
   c. schon

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Ich stehe immer um sechs Uhr auf.*

</details>

2. **Er wartet immer noch.** What's the situation?\
   a. He waits regularly.\
   b. He's been waiting a long time and hasn't stopped.\
   c. He waited once before.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** still waiting, and it's gone on longer than expected.

</details>

3. What does **immer wieder** mean?\
   a. always again\
   b. over and over\
   c. still

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** over and over, repeatedly.

</details>

4. Contrast: **Er ist noch krank.** / **Er ist immer noch krank.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Both mean he's still ill. *immer noch* adds a note of "this has been going on a while"

**Explanation:** surprise, impatience, or concern.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "the bus is always late" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Der Bus kommt immer zu spät.**

**Explanation:** *immer* expresses repetition or continuity: “always.”

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "I always drink tea in the morning"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich trinke morgens immer Tee.** (*Ich trinke immer Tee am Morgen* also works.)

**Explanation:** *immer* expresses repetition or continuity: “always.”

</details>
