## Mini Review


1. **Ich arbeite gerade.** What is the speaker saying?\
   a. I have a job.\
   b. I'm working at this moment.\
   c. I worked earlier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** working at this moment.

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Sie ist ___ gegangen.* (she just left)\
   a. gerade\
   b. schon\
   c. immer

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Sie ist gerade gegangen.*

</details>

3. Someone giving directions says **immer geradeaus**. What should you do?\
   a. Turn around.\
   b. Keep going straight.\
   c. Wait.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** keep going straight ahead.

</details>

4. Contrast: **Ich arbeite.** / **Ich arbeite gerade.** When would each be the right thing to say on the phone?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *Ich arbeite* answers "what do you do?" *Ich arbeite gerade* answers "is now a good time?"

**Explanation:** and means no.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Someone calls at a bad moment. How would you say "I'm eating right now" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich esse gerade.**

**Explanation:** *gerade* often marks something happening right now or something that has just happened.

</details>
