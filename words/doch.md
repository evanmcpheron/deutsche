**Type:** Modal particle; also the contradicting "yes"

[Category Index](./README.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

*doch* could sit in either `words/` or `particles/`. Under this repository's placement rule —

> **If a word's main A1-relevant use is as a modal particle — shaping tone or attitude rather than contributing core meaning — its canonical page lives in `particles/`. Otherwise it lives in `words/`.**

— *doch* belongs in `particles/`, because nearly everything it does at A1 is tone work: encouraging, pushing back, or reminding.

## → [Read the full page: particles/doch.md](../particles/doch.md)

The one use that isn't tone work is also covered there: *doch* as the special "yes" that contradicts a negative question.

**Kommst du nicht mit? — Doch!**
Aren't you coming? — Yes, I am!
