# Phrases

[↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md) | [README](../README.md) | [Patterns](../patterns/README.md)

Collections of real German you will hear, grouped by situation rather than by word. These pages are for **exposure** — read them the way you'd read a phrasebook, not the way you'd study a grammar table.

| Page | What's in it |
|---|---|
| [Everyday Patterns](everyday-patterns.md) | Home, food, family, shopping, the ordinary day |
| [Conversation Patterns](conversation-patterns.md) | Reacting, agreeing, hesitating, softening — where particles live |
| [Travel Patterns](travel-patterns.md) | Trains, hotels, restaurants, directions, tickets |
| [Common Chunks](common-chunks.md) | The short fixed expressions, gathered in one place |

## How These Differ from the Patterns Section

[Patterns](../patterns/README.md) explains **how a chunk works** — one page per chunk, with the logic behind it. The pages here just show you **a lot of German at once**, lightly grouped, so the same words keep passing in front of you in new company. Both matter; they do different jobs.

## Reading These Without Getting Overwhelmed

- Don't try to memorize a page. Read it once, notice two or three things, move on.
- Come back to the same page a week later. Different lines will stand out.
- Say them out loud. These are spoken phrases, and they have a rhythm.
- If a line contains a word you've never met, skip it. It'll come around again.
