# Conversation Patterns

[← Everyday Patterns](everyday-patterns.md) | [Category Index](./README.md) | [Next → Travel Patterns](travel-patterns.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

Reacting, agreeing, hesitating, softening. This is where the [particles](../particles/README.md) actually live — read this page alongside them.

## Agreeing

**Ja, klar!** — Yes, of course!
**Genau!** — Exactly!
**Eben!** — Exactly, that's what I said.
**Das stimmt.** — That's right.
**Da hast du recht.** — You're right there.
**Ich auch.** — Me too.
**Auch gut.** — That works too.

→ [eben](../particles/eben.md) · [auch](../words/auch.md)

## Disagreeing Gently

**Doch!** — Yes it is! *(contradicting a negative)*
**Eigentlich nicht.** — Not really.
**Na ja …** — Well …
**Das finde ich nicht.** — I don't think so.
**Ich weiß nicht.** — I don't know.
**Das stimmt gar nicht.** — That's not true at all.

→ [doch](../particles/doch.md) · [eigentlich](../particles/eigentlich.md) · [gar nicht](../patterns/gar-nicht.md)

## Softening a Request

**Kannst du mir mal helfen?** — Could you give me a hand?
**Warte mal.** — Hang on a sec.
**Sag mal, hast du Zeit?** — Say, have you got time?
**Haben Sie vielleicht einen Stift?** — Would you happen to have a pen?
**Ich habe nur eine Frage.** — I've just got one question.
**Ich wollte nur fragen …** — I just wanted to ask …

→ [mal](../particles/mal.md) · [vielleicht](../words/vielleicht.md) · [nur](../words/nur.md)

## Asking with Interest

**Wie geht's dir denn?** — So how are you doing?
**Was machst du denn?** — What are you up to, then?
**Warum denn nicht?** — Why not?
**Was ist denn los?** — What's going on?
**Wie alt bist du eigentlich?** — How old are you, actually?
**Was machst du eigentlich beruflich?** — So what do you do for a living?

→ [denn](../particles/denn.md) · [eigentlich](../particles/eigentlich.md)

## Encouraging

**Komm doch mit!** — Oh, come with us.
**Das schaffst du schon.** — You'll manage.
**Das wird schon.** — It'll be fine.
**Das ist doch nicht so schwer.** — Come on, it's not that hard.
**Nur zu!** — Go ahead!

→ [doch](../particles/doch.md) · [schon](../words/schon.md)

## Buying Time

**Moment mal.** — Just a moment.
**Wie sagt man das auf Deutsch?** — How do you say that in German?
**Ich verstehe nicht.** — I don't understand.
**Können Sie das noch mal sagen?** — Could you say that again?
**Etwas langsamer, bitte.** — A bit slower, please.
**Ich spreche nur ein bisschen Deutsch.** — I only speak a little German.

→ [ein bisschen](../patterns/ein-bisschen.md) · [noch](../words/noch.md)

## Resigned or Accepting

**Das ist eben so.** — That's just how it is.
**Das ist halt so.** — Same thing, more casual.
**Dann eben morgen.** — Tomorrow, then.
**Na gut.** — All right then.
**Schon gut.** — It's fine, never mind.

→ [eben](../particles/eben.md) · [halt](../particles/halt.md)

## Ending a Conversation

**Bis später!** — See you later!
**Bis bald!** — See you soon!
**Bis morgen!** — See you tomorrow!
**Wir sehen uns.** — See you around.
**Mach's gut!** — Take care!

→ [von … bis …](../patterns/von-bis.md)

## A Note on Particles Here

Most of the lines above would still be *grammatically* fine with the particle removed — *Warte* instead of *Warte mal*, *Wie geht's dir* instead of *Wie geht's dir denn*. What you'd lose is warmth. That's the whole job these words do, and reading them in bulk like this is the fastest way to start feeling it.
