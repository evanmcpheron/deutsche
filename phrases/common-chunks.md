# Common Chunks

[← Travel Patterns](travel-patterns.md) | [Category Index](README.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

Short fixed expressions, gathered in one place. Each is worth learning **as a single unit** — the meaning isn't always predictable from the parts, and assembling them word by word is slower than remembering them whole.

Chunks with their own full page are linked; the rest are explained here.

## Built on *noch*

| Chunk | Means |
|---|---|
| [noch nicht](../patterns/noch-nicht.md) | not yet |
| [noch ein …](../patterns/noch-ein.md) | another one |
| [immer noch](../patterns/immer-noch.md) | still, even now |
| **noch einmal / noch mal** | one more time |
| **noch etwas** | something else — *Noch etwas?* |
| **noch nie** | never (yet) — *Ich war noch nie dort.* |
| **nur noch** | only … left — *Nur noch zwei Minuten.* |

## Built on Prepositions + Pronouns

These come up constantly and are worth drilling until automatic:

| Chunk | Means |
|---|---|
| **bei mir / bei dir** | at my place / at yours |
| **mit mir / mit dir** | with me / with you |
| **von mir / von dir** | from me / from you |
| **für mich / für dich** | for me / for you |
| **von hier** | from here |

Note the pattern: *bei, mit, von* take **dative** (*mir, dir*), while *für* takes **accusative** (*mich, dich*). See [Cases at a Glance](../reference/cases-at-a-glance.md).

| Chunk | Means |

| **in die Schweiz** | to Switzerland |

## Conversational Fillers

| Chunk | Means |
|---|---|
| **vielleicht später** | maybe later |
| **gerade jetzt** | right at this moment |
| **na ja …** | well … |
| **also …** | so … / well … |
| **Moment mal** | just a second |
| **erst mal** | first of all |

## How to Practise These

Pick five, say them out loud, and use them in your next three sentences of German — even if the sentences are simple. Chunks stick through use, not through review.
