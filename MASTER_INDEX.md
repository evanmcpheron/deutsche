# Master Index

[← README](README.md) | [Start Here](START-HERE.md) | [Concept Index](CONCEPT-INDEX.md) | [Study Guide](STUDY_GUIDE.md) | [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md)

Every primary word covered in this repository, alphabetically, plus indexes for verb-conjugation systems and supporting material. Words with more than one function have that noted briefly.

**Tier key:** **1** = learn actively now · **2** = recognize now, produce gradually · **3** = recognition only for now. See [The A1 Priority System](reference/a1-priority-system.md).

---

## A

- [**an**](prepositions/an.md) · [quiz](prepositions/an-quiz.md) — preposition (**two-way**: dative or accusative); also a separable verb prefix — *Tier 2*
- [**auch**](words/auch.md) · [quiz](words/auch-quiz.md) — adverb ("also, too"); also used as a light emphasis particle — *Tier 1*
- [**auf**](prepositions/auf.md) · [quiz](prepositions/auf-quiz.md) — preposition (**two-way**: dative or accusative) — *Tier 1*
- [**aus**](prepositions/aus.md) · [quiz](prepositions/aus-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); origin and "out of" — *Tier 1*

## B

- [**bei**](prepositions/bei.md) · [quiz](prepositions/bei-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); "at someone's place", "near", "while" — *Tier 1*

## D

- [**denn**](particles/denn.md) · [quiz](particles/denn-quiz.md) — modal particle in questions; also a conjunction ("because") — *Tier 2*
- [**doch**](particles/doch.md) · [quiz](particles/doch-quiz.md) — modal particle; also the contradicting "yes" — *Tier 2*
  - *canonical page is in* `particles/` *— see [why](words/doch.md)*

## E

- [**eben**](particles/eben.md) · [quiz](particles/eben-quiz.md) — modal particle ("that's just how it is"); also "just now" — *Tier 3*
- [**eigentlich**](particles/eigentlich.md) · [quiz](particles/eigentlich-quiz.md) — modal particle / adverb ("actually, come to think of it") — *Tier 2*
  - *canonical page is in* `particles/` *— see [why](words/eigentlich.md)*
- [**ein bisschen**](patterns/ein-bisschen.md) · [quiz](patterns/ein-bisschen-quiz.md) — pattern ("a little / a bit") — *Tier 1*
- [**erst**](words/erst.md) · [quiz](words/erst-quiz.md) — adverb ("only, not until, first") — *Tier 2*

## F

- [**für**](prepositions/fuer.md) · [quiz](prepositions/fuer-quiz.md) — preposition (always accusative) — *Tier 1*

## G

- [**gar nicht**](patterns/gar-nicht.md) · [quiz](patterns/gar-nicht-quiz.md) — pattern (strong negation, "not at all") — *Tier 2*
- [**gerade**](words/gerade.md) · [quiz](words/gerade-quiz.md) — adverb ("right now, just"); also "straight" — *Tier 2*

## H

- [**haben**](verbs/haben.md) — irregular verb ("to have"); essential present forms and Perfekt auxiliary — *Core*
- [**halt**](particles/halt.md) · [quiz](particles/halt-quiz.md) — modal particle (resignation, "that's just how it is") — *Tier 3*

## I

- [**immer**](words/immer.md) · [quiz](words/immer-quiz.md) — adverb ("always") — *Tier 1*
- [**immer noch**](patterns/immer-noch.md) · [quiz](patterns/immer-noch-quiz.md) — pattern ("still, even now") — *Tier 1*
- [**in**](prepositions/in.md) · [quiz](prepositions/in-quiz.md) — preposition (**two-way**: dative or accusative) — *Tier 1*

## J

- [**ja**](particles/ja.md) · [quiz](particles/ja-quiz.md) — modal particle (shared knowledge, emphasis); also plain "yes" — *Tier 3 as a particle*

## K

- [**kein**](words/kein.md) · [quiz](words/kein-quiz.md) — negative article ("no, not a") — *Tier 1*

## M

- [**mal**](particles/mal.md) · [quiz](particles/mal-quiz.md) — modal particle (softening); also "times" and short for *einmal* — *Tier 2*
  - *canonical page is in* `particles/` *— see [why](words/mal.md)*
- [**mit**](prepositions/mit.md) · [quiz](prepositions/mit-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); also a separable verb prefix — *Tier 1*

## N

- [**nach**](prepositions/nach.md) · [quiz](prepositions/nach-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); destinations and "after" — *Tier 1*
- [**nicht**](words/nicht.md) · [quiz](words/nicht-quiz.md) — negation particle — *Tier 1*
- [**nicht mehr**](patterns/nicht-mehr.md) · [quiz](patterns/nicht-mehr-quiz.md) — pattern ("not any more") — *Tier 1*
- [**noch**](words/noch.md) · [quiz](words/noch-quiz.md) — adverb / particle ("still, yet, another") — *Tier 1*
- [**noch ein …**](patterns/noch-ein.md) · [quiz](patterns/noch-ein-quiz.md) — pattern ("another one") — *Tier 1*
- [**noch nicht**](patterns/noch-nicht.md) · [quiz](patterns/noch-nicht-quiz.md) — pattern ("not yet") — *Tier 1*
- [**nur**](words/nur.md) · [quiz](words/nur-quiz.md) — adverb / particle ("only, just") — *Tier 1*

## S

- [**sein**](verbs/sein.md) — irregular verb ("to be"); essential present and past forms — *Core*
- [**schon**](words/schon.md) · [quiz](words/schon-quiz.md) — adverb / particle ("already, by now"); also reassurance — *Tier 1*
- [**schon mal**](patterns/schon-mal.md) · [quiz](patterns/schon-mal-quiz.md) — pattern ("ever / before now") — *Tier 2*
- [**sehr**](words/sehr.md) · [quiz](words/sehr-quiz.md) — adverb ("very") — *Tier 1*

## U

- [**um**](prepositions/um.md) · [quiz](prepositions/um-quiz.md) — preposition (always accusative); clock times and "around" — *Tier 1*

## V

- [**vielleicht**](words/vielleicht.md) · [quiz](words/vielleicht-quiz.md) — adverb ("maybe"); also a softener — *Tier 1*
- [**von**](prepositions/von.md) · [quiz](prepositions/von-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); source, origin, "of" — *Tier 1*
- [**von … bis …**](patterns/von-bis.md) · [quiz](patterns/von-bis-quiz.md) — pattern ("from … to …") — *Tier 1*

## W

- [**werden**](verbs/werden.md) — irregular verb ("to become/get"); `du wirst`, `er wird` — *Core*
- [**wieder**](words/wieder.md) · [quiz](words/wieder-quiz.md) — adverb ("again") — *Tier 1*

## Z

- [**zu**](prepositions/zu.md) · [quiz](prepositions/zu-quiz.md) — preposition (always dative); also the adverb "too" (*zu teuer*) — *Tier 1*
- **zu Hause / nach Hause** — fixed expressions; see [zu Hause vs. nach Hause](comparisons/zu-hause-vs-nach-hause.md) · [quiz](comparisons/zu-hause-vs-nach-hause-quiz.md) — *Tier 1*
