## Mini Review


1. You've finished your beer and want a second. What do you say?\
   a. Ein anderes Bier, bitte.\
   b. Noch ein Bier, bitte.\
   c. Wieder ein Bier, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Noch ein Bier, bitte.*

</details>

2. A server asks **Noch etwas?** What are they asking?\
   a. Do you want anything else?\
   b. Is everything all right?\
   c. Would you like to pay?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** anything else?

</details>

3. Which is the careful form for a masculine noun: *noch ein Kaffee* or *noch einen Kaffee*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **noch einen Kaffee** (accusative). Both are heard; this one is the careful version.

**Explanation:** *noch ein …* asks for or describes one more of the same kind of thing.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** Order one more glass of water.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Noch ein Glas Wasser, bitte.**

**Explanation:** *noch ein …* asks for or describes one more of the same kind of thing.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Ask a friend whether they'd like another coffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Möchtest du noch einen Kaffee?**

**Explanation:** *noch ein …* asks for or describes one more of the same kind of thing.

</details>
