# Quiz: noch ein …

## Part A — Read the Situation

1. You already finished one coffee and want a second serving of the same kind. What is the natural careful order?\
   a. Wieder Kaffee, bitte.\
   b. Ein anderer Kaffee, bitte.\
   c. Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.\
   d. Noch mehr Kaffee, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** **Noch einen Kaffee, bitte** asks for one more coffee after an earlier one. *Ein anderer Kaffee* asks for a different coffee, and *noch mehr Kaffee* asks for a larger amount rather than one additional serving.

</details>

2. You have not ordered anything yet and simply want your first coffee. Which sentence is the best fit?\
   a. Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.\
   b. Noch mehr Kaffee, bitte.\
   c. Ein anderer Kaffee, bitte.\
   d. Einen Kaffee, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** For the first order, **Einen Kaffee, bitte** is enough. Adding *noch* tells the server that this is an additional serving.

</details>

3. You already have one cola and want one more. Which phrase fits?\
   a. Noch eine Cola, bitte.\
   b. Noch einen Cola, bitte.\
   c. Noch ein Cola, bitte.\
   d. Ein anderer Cola, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Cola* is feminine: **die Cola**, so the article form is **noch eine Cola**.

</details>

4. You already had one beer and want another. Which phrase fits the neuter noun **das Bier**?\
   a. Noch einen Bier, bitte.\
   b. Noch eine Bier, bitte.\
   c. Noch ein Bier, bitte.\
   d. Ein anderer Bier, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** *Bier* is neuter, so the form remains **noch ein Bier**. The article ending changes according to the noun and case.

</details>

5. There are four chairs, but five people need to sit. Which sentence fits?\
   a. Wir brauchen wieder einen Stuhl.\
   b. Wir brauchen noch einen Stuhl.\
   c. Wir brauchen noch mehr Stuhl.\
   d. Wir brauchen einen anderen Stuhl.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** You need one additional chair of the same general kind, so **noch einen Stuhl** fits. *Einen anderen Stuhl* would mean a different chair instead of the current one.

</details>

6. A server asks **Noch etwas?** What are they asking?\
   a. Is the food expensive?\
   b. Do you want the same thing again?\
   c. Are you ready to pay?\
   d. Would you like anything else?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Noch etwas?** is a common short question meaning “Anything else?” It asks whether you want something additional.

</details>

## Part B — One More, More, Different, or Again?

7. You already have two bottles of water and need two additional bottles. Which phrase fits?\
   a. Noch zwei Flaschen Wasser, bitte.\
   b. Wieder zwei Flaschen Wasser, bitte.\
   c. Zwei andere Flaschen Wasser, bitte.\
   d. Noch mehr Wasser, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** The lesson extends the pattern to numbers: **noch zwei ...** means two more. This is additional quantity, not a repeated action.

</details>

8. You did not hear a sentence clearly and want the person to say it one more time. Which sentence fits?\
   a. Sag das noch mal.\
   b. Sag noch einen Satz.\
   c. Sag einen anderen Satz.\
   d. Sag das noch mehr.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** **Noch mal** is the related pattern for “one more time.” It adds another occurrence of the action rather than another physical item.

</details>

9. Your cup is not empty. You do not want a new cup; you want a larger amount of coffee in the cup you already have. Which phrase best matches that idea?\
   a. Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.\
   b. Ein anderer Kaffee, bitte.\
   c. Wieder ein Kaffee, bitte.\
   d. Noch mehr Kaffee, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Noch mehr Kaffee** means a larger amount of coffee. **Noch einen Kaffee** normally asks for one additional serving or cup.

</details>

10. You do not want another serving of the same coffee. You want a different kind of coffee instead. Which phrase expresses that contrast?\
   a. Noch einen Kaffee.\
   b. Noch mehr Kaffee.\
   c. Einen anderen Kaffee.\
   d. Noch mal Kaffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** **Ein anderer Kaffee** means a different coffee. The lesson warns that it does not mean one more serving of the same kind.

</details>

11. What is the main lesson-taught difference between **noch einen Kaffee** and **wieder Kaffee**?\
   a. *Wieder* marks masculine accusative.\
   b. *Noch einen Kaffee* focuses on one additional serving; *wieder* focuses on an event happening again.\
   c. *Wieder* means a larger amount.\
   d. They always mean exactly the same thing.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Noch ein ...* counts an additional item or serving. *Wieder* says that an event repeats and does not by itself specify an additional serving.

</details>

12. Which is the careful form when ordering one more **der Kaffee** after verbs such as *möchten* or in the short café order?\
   a. Noch eine Kaffee.\
   b. Noch einen Kaffee.\
   c. Noch ein Kaffee.\
   d. Noch einem Kaffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Kaffee* is masculine, and the careful accusative form is **noch einen Kaffee**. The lesson notes that *noch ein Kaffee* is heard, but **noch einen Kaffee** is the careful form to learn.

</details>

13. You intend to ask for one more coffee of the same kind. A learner says **Ein anderer Kaffee, bitte.** How should you judge it?\
   a. Incorrect German.\
   b. Correct only in formal German.\
   c. Correct and exactly expresses one more serving of the same kind.\
   d. Correct German, but it asks for a different coffee instead.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Ein anderer Kaffee** is grammatical, but *anderer* changes the meaning to a different coffee. For one more serving, use **Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.**

</details>

## Part C — Form, Naturalness, and Diagnosis

14. Which sentence does **not** use the lesson's idea of an additional item, amount, or occurrence?\
   a. Noch eine Cola, bitte.\
   b. Noch zwei Bier, bitte.\
   c. Sag das noch mal.\
   d. Der Bus kommt wieder.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Der Bus kommt wieder** uses *wieder* for an event happening again. The other choices use lesson-taught *noch* patterns for something additional or one more occurrence.

</details>

15. Reconstruct the sentence from **wir / noch / einen / Stuhl / brauchen**. Which order is natural?\
   a. Wir brauchen noch einen Stuhl.\
   b. Noch wir brauchen einen Stuhl.\
   c. Wir noch einen Stuhl brauchen.\
   d. Wir brauchen einen noch Stuhl.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** **Wir brauchen noch einen Stuhl** is the natural pattern: *noch* comes before the article phrase that names the additional item.

</details>

16. A learner wants the careful café form and says **Noch ein Kaffee, bitte.** Which revision matches the lesson's recommended careful form?\
   a. Noch eine Kaffee, bitte.\
   b. Noch einem Kaffee, bitte.\
   c. Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.\
   d. Noch Kaffee einen, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** The careful masculine accusative form is **noch einen Kaffee**. The lesson notes that *noch ein Kaffee* is commonly heard, so the original is understandable rather than nonsense.

</details>

17. You want one additional beer. A learner says **Wieder ein Bier, bitte.** What is the best judgment based on this lesson?\
   a. It is the only correct way to order another beer.\
   b. It is understandable, but *noch ein Bier* is the clearer pattern for one additional serving.\
   c. It means a different kind of beer.\
   d. It means more beer in the same glass.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Wieder* focuses on something happening again. When the point is one additional serving, **noch ein Bier** expresses that directly and is the pattern taught here.

</details>

18. Your friend is pouring coffee into your cup. You want a little more, not a second cup. Which phrase best fits?\
   a. Noch einen Kaffee.\
   b. Noch ein bisschen Kaffee.\
   c. Einen anderen Kaffee.\
   d. Wieder einen Kaffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** **Noch ein bisschen Kaffee** means a little more coffee, which matches adding to the existing cup. **Noch einen Kaffee** would normally suggest another serving.

</details>

19. **Select all that apply.** Which expressions can naturally be used when asking for additional food, drink, or items?\
   a. Noch eine Cola, bitte.\
   b. Noch zwei Bier, bitte.\
   c. Noch etwas?\
   d. Sag das noch mal.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a, b, c**

**Explanation:** Options a and b request additional items, and **Noch etwas?** asks whether anything else is wanted. Option d is also a valid related *noch* pattern, but it asks for an action to happen one more time rather than for an additional food, drink, or item.

</details>

## Part D — Retrieve and Produce

20. You already have enough chairs for everyone except one person. Fill the blank with the careful form from this lesson:

**Wir brauchen ___ Stuhl.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **noch einen**

**Explanation:** *Stuhl* is masculine and is the object of *brauchen*, so the careful form is **Wir brauchen noch einen Stuhl.**

</details>

21. Start with **Einen Kaffee, bitte.** Change the situation: you have already had one and now want a second serving of the same kind. Change only what is necessary.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.**

**Explanation:** Adding *noch* changes the request from a coffee to one additional coffee while keeping the careful masculine accusative form *einen*.

</details>

22. Correct **Ein anderer Kaffee, bitte.** The intended meaning is “One more coffee of the same kind, please.”

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.**

**Explanation:** *Ein anderer Kaffee* asks for a different coffee. **Noch einen Kaffee** asks for another serving of the same kind.

</details>
