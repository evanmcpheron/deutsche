## Mini Review


1. **Er arbeitet nicht mehr hier.** What happened?\
   a. He never worked here.\
   b. He used to work here and has left.\
   c. He starts next week.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** he used to work here.

</details>

2. Choose the natural form: *Wir haben ___ Brot ___.* (we're out of bread)\
   a. nicht / mehr\
   b. kein / mehr\
   c. noch / nicht

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Wir haben kein Brot mehr.*

</details>

3. Contrast: **Er ist noch nicht hier.** / **Er ist nicht mehr hier.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *noch nicht hier* = he hasn't arrived yet, we're expecting him. *nicht mehr hier* = he was here and has gone.

**Explanation:** *nicht mehr* means “not anymore”: something was true before but has stopped.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm not tired any more" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin nicht mehr müde.**

**Explanation:** *nicht mehr* means “not anymore”: something was true before but has stopped.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "there's no time left", using *kein … mehr*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Wir haben keine Zeit mehr.** (Or *Es ist keine Zeit mehr.*)

**Explanation:** *nicht mehr* means “not anymore”: something was true before but has stopped.

</details>
