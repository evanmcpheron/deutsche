## Mini Review


1. **Warst du schon mal in Hamburg?** What's being asked?\
   a. Are you in Hamburg now?\
   b. Have you ever been to Hamburg?\
   c. Are you going to Hamburg?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** have you ever been there.

</details>

2. How do you answer "no, never" to that question?\
   a. Nein, nicht schon mal.\
   b. Nein, noch nie.\
   c. Nein, schon nicht.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Nein, noch nie.*

</details>

3. **Ich fange schon mal an.** What's the speaker doing?\
   a. Starting ahead of the others.\
   b. Refusing to start.\
   c. Asking when to start.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** going ahead and starting now.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** Ask a friend whether they've ever tried German food, using *schon mal*.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Hast du schon mal deutsches Essen probiert?**

**Explanation:** *schon mal* commonly asks or states whether something has happened before at least once.

</details>
