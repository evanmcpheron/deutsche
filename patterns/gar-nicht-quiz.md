## Mini Review


1. **Das ist gar nicht schwer.** What's the speaker doing?\
   a. Warning you.\
   b. Reassuring you.\
   c. Complaining.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** reassuring: it's easier than you think.

</details>

2. Choose the natural form: *Ich habe ___ Zeit.* (no time at all)\
   a. gar nicht\
   b. gar keine\
   c. nicht gar

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich habe gar keine Zeit.*

</details>

3. What does *gar* contribute on its own?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Nothing translatable on its own

**Explanation:** it intensifies the negation that follows.

</details>

4. **Recognition:** Is **Das gefällt mir gar nicht** positive or negative?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Negative

**Explanation:** strong dislike.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm not tired at all" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin gar nicht müde.**

**Explanation:** *gar nicht* strengthens negation to mean “not at all.”

</details>
