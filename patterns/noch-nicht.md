# noch nicht

**Type:** Pattern (chunk)
**Means:** not yet
**Level:** A1 · **Tier 1** · **Active**

[Category Index](README.md) | [Next → noch ein …](noch-ein.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

## What It Means

*noch nicht* = **not yet**. Something hasn't happened — but it's expected to.

The two halves each do their job: [noch](../words/noch.md) says "the situation is still running," [nicht](../words/nicht.md) says "not." Together: still not, i.e. not yet.

The order is fixed: **noch nicht**, never *nicht noch*.

## In Use

**Ich bin noch nicht fertig.**
I'm not finished yet.

**Der Zug ist noch nicht da.**
The train isn't here yet.

**Ich habe noch nicht gegessen.**
I haven't eaten yet.

**Wir sind noch nicht angekommen.**
We haven't arrived yet.

**Noch nicht!**
Not yet! *(a complete answer)*

## With Nouns: *noch kein*

If you're negating a noun, the *nicht* becomes *kein* — exactly as it does everywhere else ([nicht vs. kein](../comparisons/nicht-vs-kein.md)):

**Ich habe noch keine Tickets.**
I don't have tickets yet.

**Wir haben noch keinen Tisch.**
We don't have a table yet.

## Where It Sits

Same position rules as plain *nicht*: as late as possible, but before a final verb or an adjective.

**Ich bin noch nicht müde.** *(before the adjective)*
**Er kann noch nicht schwimmen.** *(before the final verb)*
**Sie hat noch nicht angerufen.** *(before the past participle)*

## The Contrast Set

Learn these three together — they're the answers to "is it done?":

| German | Situation |
|---|---|
| **Ich bin noch nicht fertig.** | Not finished — but I will be |
| **Ich bin schon fertig.** | Already finished |
| **Ich bin nicht mehr hier.** | I'm gone now — it's over |

See [nicht mehr](nicht-mehr.md) and [noch vs. schon](../comparisons/noch-vs-schon.md).

## Common Mistakes

- **Reversing it.** *nicht noch* is not the same thing — avoid it entirely at A1.
- **Using *nicht* with a noun.** *noch nicht Tickets* → **noch keine Tickets**.
- **Trying to translate *noch* separately.** In this chunk, *noch* isn't "still" in any way you can point at. Take the pair whole.
- **Reaching for *schon nicht*.** The negative of *schon* is *noch nicht*, not *schon nicht*.
