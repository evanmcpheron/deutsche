# gar nicht

**Type:** Pattern (chunk)
**Means:** not at all
**Level:** A1–A2 · **Tier 2** · **Recognition First**

## What It Means

*gar nicht* = **not at all.** It's a stronger, more emphatic [nicht](../words/nicht.md).

*gar* on its own doesn't mean anything useful to a beginner — it exists almost entirely to intensify negations. Learn the chunk, not the word.

## In Use

**Das ist gar nicht schlimm.**
That's not bad at all.

**Ich bin gar nicht müde.**
I'm not tired at all.

**Das gefällt mir gar nicht.**
I don't like that at all.

**Das stimmt gar nicht.**
That's not true at all.

## With Nouns: *gar kein*

Same split as everywhere else — nouns take *kein*:

**Ich habe gar keine Zeit.**
I have no time whatsoever.

**Das ist gar kein Problem.**
That's no problem at all.

## Relatives Worth Recognizing

- **überhaupt nicht** — not at all *(same job, very common, slightly more formal)*
- **gar nichts** — nothing at all: *Ich habe gar nichts gesagt.*
- **gar niemand** — nobody at all
- **noch gar nicht** — not at all yet: *Ich habe noch gar nicht angefangen.*

## Softening vs. Strengthening

Notice that *gar nicht* can be **reassuring** as well as emphatic, depending on the adjective:

**Das ist gar nicht schwer.**
That's not hard at all. *(encouraging)*

**Das gefällt mir gar nicht.**
I don't like that at all. *(emphatic disapproval)*

Same chunk, opposite emotional direction — the adjective decides.

## Common Mistakes

- **Trying to translate *gar*.** On its own it contributes nothing you can point at in English.
- **Using *nicht* with a noun.** *gar nicht Zeit* → **gar keine Zeit**.
- **Confusing it with *gerade*.** Different words entirely — see [gerade](../words/gerade.md).
