## Mini Review


1. **Wir haben von Montag bis Freitag geöffnet.** What's described?\
   a. A one-day event.\
   b. The days they're open.\
   c. A holiday.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** their opening days.

</details>

2. Fill in the pattern: *Der Bus fährt von hier ___ ___ Bahnhof.*

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **bis zum**

**Explanation:** *von hier bis zum Bahnhof.*

</details>

3. Someone says **Bis morgen!** What are they doing?\
   a. Saying goodbye until tomorrow.\
   b. Asking about tomorrow.\
   c. Cancelling.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** see you tomorrow.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** How would you say "from eight to five" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **von acht bis fünf.**

**Explanation:** *von … bis …* marks a range from one point to another, especially times and places.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Say goodbye to someone you'll see later today.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Bis später!**

**Explanation:** *von … bis …* marks a range from one point to another, especially times and places.

</details>
