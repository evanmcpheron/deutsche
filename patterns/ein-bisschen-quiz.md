## Part A — Recognize the Pattern

1. At a café, you want only a small amount of milk in your coffee. Which request fits best?\
   a. Ein paar Milch, bitte.\
   b. Sehr Milch, bitte.\
   c. Ein bisschen Milch, bitte.\
   d. Viele Milch, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen Milch, bitte** asks for a small amount of milk. *Milch* is not a countable plural here, so *ein paar* does not fit.

</details>

2. **Das Hotel ist ein bisschen teuer.** What does the speaker mean?\
   a. The hotel is a bit expensive.\
   b. The hotel is not expensive.\
   c. The hotel is extremely expensive.\
   d. The hotel has a few expensive rooms.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** With an adjective such as *teuer*, **ein bisschen** means “a bit / slightly.” It describes a small degree of the adjective.

</details>

3. You are meeting three friends. Which phrase naturally means “a few friends”?\
   a. ein bisschen Freunde\
   b. ein bisschen Freund\
   c. ein wenig Freunde\
   d. ein paar Freunde

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** For a small number of countable plural things, the lesson says to use **ein paar**: **ein paar Freunde**. *Ein bisschen* is not used this way with countable plurals.

</details>

4. **Ich spreche ein bisschen Deutsch.** Which description fits the sentence?\
   a. I do not speak German.\
   b. I speak some German, but only a limited amount.\
   c. I speak German very well.\
   d. I speak German with a few people.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen Deutsch** expresses a limited amount or ability: “a little German.” It does not claim fluency.

</details>

5. A server has poured some coffee, but you want a small amount more. Which phrase from the lesson fits?\
   a. Ein paar mehr, bitte.\
   b. Ein bisschen weniger, bitte.\
   c. Ein bisschen mehr, bitte.\
   d. Ein bisschen was, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen mehr, bitte** means “a little more, please.” *Weniger* would ask for less, not more.

</details>

6. Compare **Das ist teuer** with **Das ist ein bisschen teuer**. What does *ein bisschen* do in the second sentence?\
   a. It makes the judgment less strong by saying “a bit expensive.”\
   b. It changes the sentence to “not expensive.”\
   c. It changes *teuer* into a noun.\
   d. It means there are a few expensive things.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen** can soften an adjective by limiting its degree. The second sentence is less blunt because it says the thing is only “a bit” expensive.

</details>

## Part B — Form and Related Patterns

7. Which statement about the form **ein bisschen** is correct?\
   a. It changes to *eine bisschen* before feminine nouns.\
   b. It stays **ein bisschen** before nouns such as *Milch*, *Zeit*, and *Geld*.\
   c. It changes to *einen bisschen* before masculine nouns.\
   d. It is used only before adjectives.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** The lesson emphasizes that **ein bisschen does not decline**. The chunk stays the same: *ein bisschen Milch*, *ein bisschen Zeit*, *ein bisschen Geld*.

</details>

8. A learner writes **Ich brauche eine bisschen Zeit.** What is the best correction?\
   a. Ich brauche ein paar Zeit.\
   b. Ich brauche einen bisschen Zeit.\
   c. Ich brauche bisschene Zeit.\
   d. Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen** stays unchanged, so the natural sentence is **Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.** The gender of *Zeit* does not change the chunk.

</details>

9. You think there is too much sugar in your coffee and want a small amount less. Which phrase fits?\
   a. Ein bisschen weniger Zucker, bitte.\
   b. Ein bisschen mehr Zucker, bitte.\
   c. Ein paar weniger Zucker, bitte.\
   d. Sehr wenig Zucker mehr, bitte.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen weniger** means “a little less.” This is the opposite of *ein bisschen mehr*.

</details>

10. Which expression does the lesson give as a slightly more formal way to say roughly the same thing as **ein bisschen**?\
   a. ein paar\
   b. sehr\
   c. ein wenig\
   d. viele

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** **Ein wenig** is taught as a related expression meaning “a little,” with a slightly more formal feel. *Ein paar* is for a few countable items.

</details>

11. In **etwas Milch**, what does **etwas** do according to the lesson?\
   a. It means “many cartons of milk.”\
   b. It expresses some or a little milk.\
   c. It means the milk is expensive.\
   d. It turns *Milch* into a plural noun.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** The lesson lists **etwas** as a related way to express “some / a little,” as in **etwas Milch**.

</details>

12. Which expression is taught for recognition as a colloquial way to say “a little something”?\
   a. ein bisschen mehr\
   b. ein paar was\
   c. ein wenig mehr\
   d. ein bisschen was

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen was** is the colloquial recognition pattern given in the lesson for “a little something.”

</details>

13. Which spelling is correct?\
   a. ein bischen\
   b. ein bissschen\
   c. ein bisschen\
   d. ein bischnen

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** The lesson's correct spelling is **bisschen**. The other forms are misspellings.

</details>

## Part C — Contrast, Naturalness, and Diagnosis

14. You want to say that you bought **a few apples**. Which phrase is the natural choice?\
   a. ein paar Äpfel\
   b. ein bisschen Äpfel\
   c. ein bisschen Apfel\
   d. ein wenig Äpfel

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Äpfel* are countable plural items, so use **ein paar Äpfel** for “a few apples.” The lesson warns against *ein bisschen + countable plural*.

</details>

15. The intended idea is “I am **a little** tired.” A learner says **Ich bin müde.** How should you judge it?\
   a. Incorrect German.\
   b. It means “I have a few tired people.”\
   c. It means “I am not tired.”\
   d. Correct German, but it does not express the small degree given by *ein bisschen*.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **d**

**Explanation:** **Ich bin müde** is correct, but it simply says “I am tired.” **Ich bin ein bisschen müde** specifically limits the degree to “a little tired.”

</details>

16. Which sentence is the natural way to say that the room is slightly cold?\
   a. Es ist ein paar kalt hier.\
   b. Es ist ein bisschen kalt hier.\
   c. Es ist eine bisschen kalt hier.\
   d. Es ist bisschene kalt hier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** Before an adjective such as *kalt*, use the unchanged chunk **ein bisschen**: **Es ist ein bisschen kalt hier.**

</details>

17. Which situation calls for **ein paar** rather than **ein bisschen**?\
   a. You want a small amount of milk.\
   b. The room is slightly cold.\
   c. You are inviting a few friends.\
   d. You need a small amount of time.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

**Explanation:** Friends are countable plural people, so “a few friends” uses **ein paar Freunde**. The other situations naturally use *ein bisschen* for a small quantity or degree.

</details>

18. Reconstruct the sentence from **ich / brauche / ein bisschen / Zeit**. Which order is natural?\
   a. Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.\
   b. Ich ein bisschen brauche Zeit.\
   c. Ein bisschen ich Zeit brauche.\
   d. Ich brauche Zeit ein bisschen.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** The natural sentence is **Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.** The chunk directly expresses a small amount of *Zeit*.

</details>

19. **Select all that apply.** Which sentences use **ein bisschen** correctly according to the lesson?\
   a. Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.\
   b. Ich bin ein bisschen müde.\
   c. Nur ein bisschen Zucker, bitte.\
   d. Ich habe ein bisschen Freunde hier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a, b, c**

**Explanation:** Options a and c use *ein bisschen* for a small quantity, and b uses it for a small degree. Option d incorrectly uses it with the countable plural *Freunde*; use **ein paar Freunde** instead.

</details>

## Part D — Retrieve and Produce

20. You know only a limited amount of German. Complete the lesson's useful travel sentence:

**Ich spreche ___ Deutsch.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **ein bisschen**

**Explanation:** **Ich spreche ein bisschen Deutsch** means “I speak a little German.”

</details>

21. Correct the sentence:

**Ich brauche eine bisschen Zeit.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich brauche ein bisschen Zeit.**

**Explanation:** **Ein bisschen** does not decline. It stays unchanged before *Zeit*.

</details>

22. Soften **Das ist teuer.** so that you say it is only a bit expensive. Change only what is needed.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Das ist ein bisschen teuer.**

**Explanation:** Adding **ein bisschen** limits the degree of *teuer* and makes the statement less strong.

</details>
