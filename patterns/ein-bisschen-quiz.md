## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural form: *Ich spreche ___ Deutsch.*\
   a. ein bisschen\
   b. ein paar\
   c. sehr

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Ich spreche ein bisschen Deutsch.*

</details>

2. Which is right for "a few friends"?\
   a. ein bisschen Freunde\
   b. ein paar Freunde

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *ein paar Freunde.* Countable plurals take *ein paar*.

</details>

3. Why might you add *ein bisschen* to **Das ist teuer**?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** To soften the judgement

**Explanation:** it turns a verdict into a comment, and leaves room to negotiate or disagree politely.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm a little tired" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin ein bisschen müde.**

**Explanation:** *ein bisschen* means “a little/a bit” and softens the amount or degree being described.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Ask for a little milk in your coffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ein bisschen Milch, bitte.**

**Explanation:** *ein bisschen* means “a little/a bit” and softens the amount or degree being described.

</details>
