## Mini Review


1. **Der Bus ist noch nicht da.** What's the situation?\
   a. The bus has left.\
   b. The bus hasn't arrived yet.\
   c. The bus isn't running today.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** it hasn't arrived yet.

</details>

2. Choose the natural form: *Ich habe ___ Zeit gehabt.* (haven't had time yet)\
   a. noch nicht\
   b. noch keine\
   c. nicht noch

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich habe noch keine Zeit gehabt.* (*Zeit* is a noun → *kein*.)

</details>

3. What's the negative of **Ich bin schon fertig**?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin noch nicht fertig.**

**Explanation:** *noch nicht* is the fixed pattern for “not yet”: the expected change has not happened, but it may happen later.

</details>

4. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm not finished yet" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin noch nicht fertig.**

**Explanation:** *noch nicht* is the fixed pattern for “not yet”: the expected change has not happened, but it may happen later.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "we don't have a room yet", using *noch kein*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Wir haben noch kein Zimmer.**

**Explanation:** *noch nicht* is the fixed pattern for “not yet”: the expected change has not happened, but it may happen later.

</details>
