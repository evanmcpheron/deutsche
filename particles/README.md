# Particles

[↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md) | [README](../README.md) | [Modal Particles Overview](../reference/modal-particles-overview.md)

Modal particles are small, unstressed words that change how a sentence **sounds** rather than what it **says**. Remove one and the sentence stays true — it just becomes blunter, colder, or more neutral.

Read [Modal Particles Overview](../reference/modal-particles-overview.md) before the individual pages.

| Word | What it mostly does | Tier | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| [mal](./mal.md) · [Quiz](mal-quiz.md) | makes a request casual and small | 2 | Recognition First |
| [denn](./denn.md) · [Quiz](denn-quiz.md) | makes a question sound interested, not interrogating | 2 | Recognition First |
| [doch](./doch.md) · [Quiz](doch-quiz.md) | pushes back, encourages, or contradicts | 2 | Recognition First |
| [eigentlich](./eigentlich.md) · [Quiz](eigentlich-quiz.md) | "actually / come to think of it" | 2 | Recognition First |
| [ja](ja.md) · [Quiz](ja-quiz.md) | "as we both know" — shared knowledge | 3 | Passive |
| [eben](eben.md) · [Quiz](eben-quiz.md) | "that's just how it is" | 3 | Passive |
| [halt](halt.md) · [Quiz](halt-quiz.md) | same as *eben*, more southern and casual | 3 | Passive |

Also in this family, and worth recognizing without study at A1: **wohl** (a guess — *Er ist wohl krank*, "he's probably ill"), **schon** as reassurance (*Das wird schon*, "it'll be fine"), and **nur** in warnings (*Mach das nur nicht!*). Those live on their own pages: [schon](../words/schon.md), [nur](../words/nur.md).

## Canonical Pages

*doch*, *mal*, and *eigentlich* have their **full pages here**, not in `words/`, under the repository-wide rule:

> **If a word's main A1-relevant use is as a modal particle — shaping tone or attitude rather than contributing core meaning — its canonical page lives in `particles/`. Otherwise it lives in [`words/`](../words/README.md).**

The `words/` folder holds a short stub for each of the three, pointing here. Nothing is duplicated.

## A Warning Worth Repeating

Particles are the last thing to start producing, not the first. A learner who sprinkles *doch* and *mal* into sentences after reading about them for an afternoon reliably sounds odd. Aim to **notice** them for several months before you aim to use them.
