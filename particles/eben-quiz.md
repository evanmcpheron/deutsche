## Mini Review


1. **Das ist eben so.** What's the speaker's attitude?\
   a. Angry.\
   b. Resigned; accepting.\
   c. Surprised.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** resigned acceptance.

</details>

2. Someone says **Eben!** after your remark. What do they mean?\
   a. They disagree.\
   b. They strongly agree.\
   c. They didn't hear you.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** strong agreement, "exactly."

</details>

3. **Er war eben hier.** What does *eben* mean here?\
   a. That's just how it is.\
   b. A moment ago.\
   c. Flat.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** a moment ago. That's the adverb use, not the particle.

</details>

4. Which is more southern and casual — *eben* or *halt*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *halt*

**Explanation:** though *eben* is understood everywhere and both are common.

</details>

5. **Recognition:** Should you produce *eben* as a particle at A1?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** No, apart from the one-word *Eben!* Recognition is enough for now.

**Explanation:** *eben* commonly presents something as simply the way things are, often with acceptance or finality.

</details>
