## What Is Deliberately Not Here

This is not a grammar course. You will not find:

- Full declension tables for adjectives
- The genitive case
- Verb tenses beyond what the examples need
- *Konjunktiv*, passive voice, or relative clauses

Those matter later. Nothing in this repository requires them, and every example here has been kept simple enough that the small word — not the grammar around it — is the hard part.
