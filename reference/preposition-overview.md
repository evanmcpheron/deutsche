# Preposition Overview

[← Active vs. Passive Vocabulary](active-vs-passive-vocabulary.md) | [Category Index](./README.md) | [Next → Cases at a Glance](cases-at-a-glance.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

How to choose between German prepositions when English gives you only one word to work with.

## The Core Problem

English "to" is doing at least four jobs that German splits:

| English | German | Example |
|---|---|---|
| to (a person) | **zu** | zum Arzt |
| to (a city, country) | **nach** | nach Berlin |
| to (a country with an article) | **in** + accusative | in die Schweiz |
| to (inside a building) | **in** + accusative | ins Kino |

And English "from" splits in two:

| English | German | Example |
|---|---|---|
| from (origin, where you belong) | **aus** | aus Deutschland |
| from (today's starting point, a person) | **von** | von der Arbeit, von Anna |

Learning German prepositions is therefore not vocabulary work. It's **sorting** — learning which German word covers which slice of the English word's territory.

## Destination Decision Table

When you want to say "I'm going to ___":

| The destination is … | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a person | **zu** | zu meiner Mutter, zum Arzt |
| a shop, office, station | **zu** | zum Supermarkt, zur Post |
| a named city | **nach** | nach Berlin |
| a country with no article | **nach** | nach Italien |
| a country with an article | **in** + acc. | in die Schweiz |
| a building you go inside | **in** + acc. | ins Kino, ins Restaurant |
| home | **nach** | nach Hause |
| a direction | **nach** | nach links |
| an errand place like the post office or market | **auf** *(idiomatic)* | auf die Post, auf den Markt |

**The one reliable rule: people always take *zu*.** Everything else is a sorting job, and some of it — the *auf die Post* group — is simply idiomatic. Don't build a theory for it; learn the phrases.

## Location vs. Destination

Many pairs differ only by which preposition you pick:

| Being there | Going there |
|---|---|
| **bei** meiner Schwester | **zu** meiner Schwester |
| **zu** Hause | **nach** Hause |
| **im** Kino | **ins** Kino |
| **in der** Stadt | **in die** Stadt |
| **auf dem** Tisch | **auf den** Tisch |

Notice the two different mechanisms: with *bei/zu* and *zu Hause/nach Hause*, the **preposition itself** changes. With *in* and *auf*, the preposition stays and the **case** changes. See [Cases at a Glance](cases-at-a-glance.md).

## The Case Summary

| Always dative | Always accusative | Two-way |
|---|---|---|
| [mit](../prepositions/mit.md), [zu](../prepositions/zu.md), [nach](../prepositions/nach.md), [bei](../prepositions/bei.md), [aus](../prepositions/aus.md), [von](../prepositions/von.md), seit | [für](../prepositions/fuer.md), [um](../prepositions/um.md), durch, ohne, gegen | [in](../prepositions/in.md), [auf](../prepositions/auf.md), [an](../prepositions/an.md), über, unter, vor, hinter, neben, zwischen |

Two memory hooks that many learners find useful — the dative group **mit, nach, bei, seit, von, zu, aus** and the accusative group **durch, für, gegen, ohne, um** are often chanted as lists. They're worth chanting: the fixed-case prepositions are pure memory work, and once done, done.

## Contractions

Completely normal German, not shortcuts:

| Full | Short | Example |
|---|---|---|
| zu dem | **zum** | zum Bahnhof |
| zu der | **zur** | zur Arbeit |
| in dem | **im** | im Kino |
| in das | **ins** | ins Kino |
| an dem | **am** | am Montag |
| an das | **ans** | ans Fenster |
| bei dem | **beim** | beim Arzt |
| von dem | **vom** | vom Bahnhof |

**A very useful side effect:** with two-way prepositions, the contraction tells you the case at a glance. *im* and *am* are dative → location. *ins* and *ans* are accusative → movement.

## Time Expressions

| Expression | Preposition |
|---|---|
| clock time | **um** acht Uhr |
| day | **am** Montag |
| part of day | **am** Abend |
| month, season | **im** Juli, **im** Sommer |
| duration "in X time" | **in** einer Stunde |
| span | **von** … **bis** … |
| "since" | **seit** einem Jahr |

## The Four Errors Worth Fixing First

1. ***nach* with a person** → use **zu**
2. ***von* for your home country** → use **aus**
3. ***bei* meaning "together with"** → use **mit**
4. **Dative for movement with *in/auf/an*** → use accusative
