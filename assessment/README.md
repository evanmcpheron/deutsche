# German A1 Progress Assessment

**Assessment Version:** `A1-WRITTEN-20260817-F2F2`  
**Blueprint Version:** `A1-Written-1.0`

## Purpose

This assessment is designed to measure your current German A1 ability in reading, writing, vocabulary, grammar, functional written communication, and related written language skills. It deliberately excludes speaking and listening. Complete it without outside help so that later attempts can show meaningful progress.

The strongest conclusion this assessment can support is **A1 Achieved in Assessed Domains**. It cannot establish full four-skill CEFR A1 proficiency because speaking and listening are not assessed.

## What Is Assessed

- reading comprehension;
- written production;
- grammar and sentence structure in use;
- vocabulary recognition and retrieval;
- functional written communication and basic pragmatics;
- spelling, noun capitalization, punctuation, umlauts, and `ß`;
- recognition, controlled production, and independent production;
- practical interpretation of ordinary written information;
- a separate, non-penalizing A2 ceiling section.

## What Is Not Assessed

There are no listening, speaking, pronunciation, oral-interaction, phonetic-discrimination, or transcript-as-listening tasks.

## Closed-Resource Rules

Do not use any outside help while an administration is unfinished. This includes:

- dictionaries;
- Google or other web searches;
- translation software such as DeepL;
- grammar references or textbooks;
- class notes, personal notes, or flashcards;
- ChatGPT or another AI assistant;
- another person;
- answer-checking tools;
- German predictive text, grammar suggestions, AI writing suggestions, or translation suggestions.

Avoid typing assistance that actively reveals German spelling. The purpose is to measure what you can produce yourself.

## German Spelling Standard

Use normal Standard German spelling. When standard spelling requires `ä`, `ö`, `ü`, or `ß`, type that character. `ae`, `oe`, `ue`, and `ss` are not automatically accepted as keyboard substitutes.

Ordinary words that correctly use `ss` still use `ss`.

## If You Do Not Know

Do not research the answer. Write:

`IDK`

or leave the designated answer blank. `IDK` is never penalized more heavily than an incorrect attempt.

## Multiple Sittings

The assessment is designed to be completed in several Markdown files. Ideally finish one version within a few days.

**Do not intentionally study German between sections of the same administration.** If unavoidable time passes between sittings, record it in `00-administration.md`. Natural exposure during ordinary life is fine, but do not use the unfinished assessment as a study guide before continuing.

Each section has a suggested time. You may finish a section after the suggested time; record the actual time and whether you exceeded the suggestion. Speed is not a major part of the proficiency score, but comparable timing makes retests easier to interpret.

Suggested total active time, including the optional A2 ceiling: about **175 minutes**.

## Confidence Ratings

A selected subset of questions asks you to mark:

- Guess
- Somewhat sure
- Certain

Choose the one that best reflects how confident you were **before checking anything**. Confidence does not change your score. It is used only to diagnose fragile knowledge and possible misconceptions.

## Do Not Open `grader-only/`

The `grader-only/` directory contains answer keys, scoring rules, the assessment blueprint, and grading instructions. Reading those materials before you finish the assessment invalidates the attempt as an unaided progress measurement.

## What to Submit for Grading

After finishing, upload:

- the completed `assessment/` Markdown files, including `00-administration.md` and `99-submission-checklist.md`;
- the entire `grader-only/` directory for the grader AI;
- optionally, previous standardized `German A1 Progress Report` files if you want longitudinal comparison.

You do not need to explain the scoring system again. `grader-only/grading-instructions.md` contains the grading procedure.

## Retakes

For a formal progress comparison, do **not** erase your answers and repeat this exact version.

1. Generate a **new equivalent version** using the same assessment-generation specification and blueprint.
2. Complete the new version under conditions as comparable as practical to this attempt.
3. Submit the completed new assessment and its matching `grader-only/` files for grading.
4. Also provide previous standardized `German A1 Progress Report` files when available.
5. Compare normalized domain, ability-mode, vocabulary, and supported subtopic results across attempts.

The new version should change the questions, names, situations, numbers, texts, vocabulary samples, and writing prompts while preserving the scoring design and coverage distribution.

You may later revisit this version for study, but once you do, it is no longer suitable as an unbiased progress retest.
