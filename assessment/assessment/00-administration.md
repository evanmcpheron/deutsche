## Administration Rules

- Work closed-resource.
- Do not intentionally study German between sections of this same assessment administration.
- If you do not know an answer, write `IDK` or leave the answer blank.
- Do not research an unknown answer.
- Type correct German characters where standard spelling requires them: `ä`, `ö`, `ü`, `ß`.
- Record actual timing. You may continue after a suggested time limit, but note that you exceeded it.
- Do not open `grader-only/` until the assessment is complete.
- You may use real or fictional personal details in any writing task.
