# 06 — Independent Writing

**Suggested time:** 35 minutes

Write in German. Do not use a dictionary, translator, notes, predictive German text, or AI assistance.

You may use **real or fictional details**. The assessment measures your German, not your personal disclosure.

Do not try to write beyond what you can genuinely produce. Simple, clear A1 German is appropriate.

---

## W001 — Short Personal Answers

Answer all five prompts in German. Write at least one complete sentence for each.

1. Wie heißen Sie oder wie heißt Ihre erfundene Person?
2. Wo wohnen Sie?
3. Was machen Sie beruflich oder was studieren Sie?
4. Was machen Sie gern in Ihrer Freizeit?
5. Was essen oder trinken Sie gern?

## W002 — Practical Message

You have an appointment with **Frau Berger on Tuesday at 15:00**, but you cannot come.

Write a short German message of about **30–45 words**. Include all of these points:

- greet Frau Berger appropriately;
- say you cannot come on Tuesday at 15:00;
- give a simple reason;
- ask for a new appointment on Thursday.

## W003 — Familiar Description

Write **6–8 simple German sentences** about a normal weekday. You may describe your real routine or invent one.

Include:

- when the day starts;
- work or study;
- one meal;
- one afternoon or evening activity;
- one time expression;
- at least one preference or frequency expression.

## W004 — Functional Written Communication

You want to spend a weekend in Hamburg and are writing to a small hotel you do not know.

- an appropriate opening;
- say when you want to stay;
- ask for a room for two people;
- ask the price;
