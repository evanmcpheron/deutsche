## Self-Check


1. Your friend was ill last month, recovered, and is now ill again. Which is right?\
   a. Er ist noch krank.\
   b. Er ist wieder krank.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Er ist wieder krank.*

</details>

2. **Ich bin wieder da.** What happened?\
   a. I never left.\
   b. I left and came back.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** left and came back.

</details>

3. What's the difference between *immer noch* and *immer wieder*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *immer noch* = still, one unbroken situation. *immer wieder* = over and over, separate repeated events.

**Explanation:** *noch* means the situation never stopped; *wieder* means it stopped or occurred before and is happening again.

</details>

4. Choose the natural word: *Es regnet ___ — den ganzen Tag schon.* (still, all day)\
   a. noch\
   b. wieder

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Es regnet noch* (it hasn't stopped all day).

</details>

5. **Produce it:** The shop was closed and has reopened. Say so in German.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Das Geschäft ist wieder offen.**

**Explanation:** *noch* means the situation never stopped; *wieder* means it stopped or occurred before and is happening again.

</details>
