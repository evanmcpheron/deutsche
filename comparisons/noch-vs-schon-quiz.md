## Self-Check


1. Choose the natural word: *Der Zug ist ___ nicht da.* (not here yet)\
   a. schon\
   b. noch

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Der Zug ist noch nicht da.*

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Es ist ___ acht Uhr — wir müssen los!* (already eight)\
   a. noch\
   b. schon

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Es ist schon acht Uhr.*

</details>

3. **Wohnst du noch in Berlin?** What's being asked?\
   a. Have you moved there yet?\
   b. Do you still live there?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** do you still live there?

</details>

4. What's the negative of **Ich bin schon fertig**?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin noch nicht fertig.**

**Explanation:** Not *schon nicht*.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "he's still asleep" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Er schläft noch.**

**Explanation:** *noch* presents something as continuing or not yet changed; *schon* presents it as already true or reached.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "I've already eaten"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich habe schon gegessen.**

**Explanation:** *noch* presents something as continuing or not yet changed; *schon* presents it as already true or reached.

</details>
