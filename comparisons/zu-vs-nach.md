# zu vs. nach

**Compares:** [zu](../prepositions/zu.md) · [nach](../prepositions/nach.md)
**Level:** A1 · **Tier 1**

[← sehr vs. viel](sehr-vs-viel.md) | [Category Index](README.md) | [Next → in vs. nach](in-vs-nach.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

## The Simple Explanation

Both translate as English "to." The choice depends on **what kind of destination** it is.

> **zu** — people, and most everyday buildings and errands.
> **nach** — cities, most countries, compass directions, and *Hause*.

There's no deep logic to reconstruct here. It's a sorting job, and the sorting is learnable in an afternoon.

## Contrasting Examples

**Ich gehe zu meiner Mutter.**
I'm going to my mother's. *(a person → zu)*

**Ich fahre nach München.**
I'm going to Munich. *(a city → nach)*

---

**Ich gehe zum Arzt.**
I'm going to the doctor. *(a person → zu)*

**Wir fliegen nach Italien.**
We're flying to Italy. *(a country → nach)*

---

**Wie komme ich zum Bahnhof?**
How do I get to the station? *(a building → zu)*

**Gehen Sie nach links.**
Go left. *(a direction → nach)*

---

**Ich bin zu Hause.**
I'm at home. *(fixed expression, location)*

**Ich gehe nach Hause.**
I'm going home. *(fixed expression, movement)*

## Rule of Thumb

| Destination | Preposition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a person | **zu** | zu meiner Schwester, zum Arzt |
| a shop, station, office | **zu** | zum Supermarkt, zur Post |
| a city | **nach** | nach Berlin |
| a country with no article | **nach** | nach Deutschland |
| a country with an article | **in** | in die Schweiz → [in vs. nach](in-vs-nach.md) |
| a direction | **nach** | nach rechts, nach oben |
| home (going) | **nach** | nach Hause |
| home (being) | **zu** | zu Hause |

**The one rule that never fails: people always take *zu*.**

## Common Mistakes

- ***Ich gehe nach meiner Mutter.*** → **zu meiner Mutter.** With a person, *nach* means "after," not "to."
- ***Ich gehe nach dem Supermarkt.*** → **zum Supermarkt.** Otherwise you've said "after the supermarket."
- ***Ich fahre zu Berlin.*** → **nach Berlin.**
- **Swapping the *Hause* pair.** *zu Hause* = at home. *nach Hause* = homeward. See [zu Hause vs. nach Hause](zu-hause-vs-nach-hause.md).

## Self-Check

1. Choose the natural word: *Ich fahre ___ Hamburg.*\
   a. zu\
   b. nach

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich gehe ___ Zahnarzt.*\
   a. zum\
   b. nach

3. Choose the natural word: *Wir fliegen ___ Spanien.*\
   a. zu\
   b. nach

4. Which destination type always takes *zu*, without exception?

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm going to my brother's" in German?

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "how do I get to the station?"
