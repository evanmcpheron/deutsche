## Self-Check


1. Choose the natural form: *Wir fahren ___ Österreich.*\
   a. nach\
   b. in die

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *nach Österreich* (no article).

</details>

2. Choose the natural form: *Wir fahren ___ Schweiz.*\
   a. nach\
   b. in die

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *in die Schweiz* (*die Schweiz* has an article; movement → accusative).

</details>

3. What's the difference between **Ich fahre in die Schweiz** and **Ich wohne in der Schweiz**?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** The first is movement (accusative, *die*): I'm travelling there. The second is location (dative, *der*): I live there.

**Explanation:** *nach* is usual with cities and most countries without articles; *in* is used for entering places and with countries that take an article.

</details>

4. Which four countries at A1 need *in* rather than *nach*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *die Schweiz*, *die Türkei*, *die USA*, *die Niederlande*

**Explanation:** plus any other country whose name comes with an article.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm flying to Italy" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich fliege nach Italien.**

**Explanation:** *nach* is usual with cities and most countries without articles; *in* is used for entering places and with countries that take an article.

</details>
