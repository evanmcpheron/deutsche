## Self-Check


1. **Es ist erst sieben Uhr.** What's the speaker suggesting?\
   a. Hurry up.\
   b. There's plenty of time.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** plenty of time.

</details>

2. **Der Bus kommt erst um neun.** Can you catch it at half past eight?\
   a. Yes.\
   b. No.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** no; *erst um neun* means not before nine.

</details>

3. Choose the natural word: *Ich bin ___ seit gestern hier.* (only since yesterday)\
   a. schon\
   b. erst

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich bin erst seit gestern hier.*

</details>

4. Contrast: **Wir warten schon seit einer Stunde.** / **Wir warten erst seit einer Stunde.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Same hour of waiting. *schon* = an hour already, that's too long. *erst* = only an hour so far, be patient.

**Explanation:** *erst* often frames a time or amount as later/less than expected; *schon* frames it as already/earlier or more than expected.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** The shop opens at ten and it's nine now. Tell someone, using *erst*.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Das Geschäft öffnet erst um zehn.**

**Explanation:** *erst* often frames a time or amount as later/less than expected; *schon* frames it as already/earlier or more than expected.

</details>
