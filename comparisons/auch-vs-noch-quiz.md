## Self-Check


1. Your friend orders a beer. You want one as well. What do you say?\
   a. Ich möchte auch ein Bier.\
   b. Ich möchte noch ein Bier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *auch*, because a different person is joining in.

</details>

2. You've finished your beer and want a second. What do you say?\
   a. Ich möchte auch ein Bier.\
   b. Ich möchte noch ein Bier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *noch*, because it's one more of the same for you.

</details>

3. Someone says *Ich bin müde*. How do you say "me too"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich auch.**

**Explanation:** *auch* adds another person or parallel item (“too/also”); *noch* can add another instance of the same thing (“another/more”).

</details>

4. Contrast: **Ich habe auch Zeit.** / **Ich habe noch Zeit.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *auch Zeit* = I have time as well as you do. *noch Zeit* = I still have time left.

**Explanation:** *auch* adds another person or parallel item (“too/also”); *noch* can add another instance of the same thing (“another/more”).

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Tell a server you'd like another glass of water.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Noch ein Glas Wasser, bitte.**

**Explanation:** *auch* adds another person or parallel item (“too/also”); *noch* can add another instance of the same thing (“another/more”).

</details>
