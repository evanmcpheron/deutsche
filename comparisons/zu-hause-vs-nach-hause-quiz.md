## Self-Check


1. You're sitting on your sofa. Which is right?\
   a. Ich bin zu Hause.\
   b. Ich bin nach Hause.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *Ich bin zu Hause.*

</details>

2. You're leaving the office. Which is right?\
   a. Ich gehe zu Hause.\
   b. Ich gehe nach Hause.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich gehe nach Hause.*

</details>

3. Fill in the pattern: *Wir bleiben heute ___.* (staying home)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **zu Hause**

**Explanation:** *Wir bleiben heute zu Hause.*

</details>

4. Which question does *nach Hause* answer — *Wo?* or *Wohin?*

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *Wohin?*

**Explanation:** it's movement toward home.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Ask a friend when they're coming home.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Wann kommst du nach Hause?**

**Explanation:** *zu Hause* describes being at home; *nach Hause* describes movement toward home.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** Say that he isn't at home.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Er ist nicht zu Hause.**

**Explanation:** *zu Hause* describes being at home; *nach Hause* describes movement toward home.

</details>
