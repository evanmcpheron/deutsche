# in vs. nach

**Compares:** [in](../prepositions/in.md) · [nach](../prepositions/nach.md)
**Level:** A1 · **Tier 1**

## The Simple Explanation

For travelling to a place:

> **nach** — cities and countries used **without an article**. The great majority.
> **in** — countries and regions that come **with an article**, and places you go *inside* (the cinema, town, the mountains).

The tiny complication: *in* for movement takes the **accusative**, so *die Schweiz* becomes *in die Schweiz*.

## Contrasting Examples

**Ich fahre nach Deutschland.**
I'm going to Germany. *(no article)*

**Ich fahre in die Schweiz.**
I'm going to Switzerland. *(die Schweiz → accusative)*

---

**Wir fliegen nach Frankreich.**
We're flying to France.

**Wir fliegen in die Türkei.**
We're flying to Turkey. *(die Türkei)*

---

**Ich fahre nach Berlin.**
I'm going to Berlin. *(a city)*

**Ich gehe in die Stadt.**
I'm going into town. *(a general place, not a name)*

---

**Ich fahre nach Hause.**
I'm going home.

**Ich gehe ins Kino.**
I'm going to the cinema. *(a building you go inside)*

## Rule of Thumb

1. **A named city → *nach*.** *nach Berlin, nach Wien, nach Zürich.*
2. **A country with no article → *nach*.** *nach Deutschland, nach Italien, nach Japan.*
3. **A country or region with an article → *in* + accusative.** *in die Schweiz, in die Türkei, in die USA.*
4. **A building or general place you enter → *in* + accusative.** *ins Kino, in die Stadt, in die Berge.*

The article-countries are a short list at A1 — *die Schweiz*, *die Türkei*, *die USA*, *die Niederlande*. Learn those four and treat everything else as *nach*.

## Location vs. Movement

Once you've **arrived**, *in* switches to dative:

**Ich fahre in die Schweiz.** *(movement → accusative)*
**Ich wohne in der Schweiz.** *(location → dative)*

**Ich gehe ins Kino.** *(movement)*
**Ich bin im Kino.** *(location)*

See [in](../prepositions/in.md) and [Cases at a Glance](../reference/cases-at-a-glance.md).

## Common Mistakes

- ***Ich fahre nach der Schweiz.*** → **in die Schweiz.**
- ***Ich fahre in Berlin*** *(meaning "to Berlin")* → **nach Berlin.** *In Berlin* means you're already there.
- **Using dative for movement.** *Ich fahre in der Schweiz* means you're driving around inside Switzerland.
- **Forgetting *zu* exists.** For shops, doctors, and people, neither of these is right — see [zu vs. nach](zu-vs-nach.md).
