# gerade vs. jetzt

**Compares:** [gerade](../words/gerade.md) · *jetzt*
**Level:** A1–A2 · **Tier 2**

[← auch vs. noch](auch-vs-noch.md) | [Category Index](./README.md) | [Next → nicht vs. kein](nicht-vs-kein.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

## The Simple Explanation

Both point at the present, but from different angles.

> **jetzt** — **now**, as opposed to earlier or later. Often marks a change: *from now on*.
> **gerade** — **at this exact moment**, usually mid-action. Often means "I'm in the middle of something."

*jetzt* puts a point on the timeline. *gerade* describes what's happening at that point.

## Contrasting Examples

**Ich arbeite jetzt.**
I'm working now. *(as opposed to before — maybe I've just started)*

**Ich arbeite gerade.**
I'm working right now. *(I'm in the middle of it — bad moment)*

---

**Was machst du jetzt?**
What are you doing now? / What will you do now?

**Was machst du gerade?**
What are you up to right this second?

---

**Jetzt gehen wir.**
Now we're going. *(let's go — a decision)*

**Wir gehen gerade.**
We're leaving right now. *(in progress)*

---

**Er ist jetzt in Berlin.**
He's in Berlin now. *(he lives there these days)*

**Er ist gerade in Berlin.**
He's in Berlin at the moment. *(a temporary trip)*

## Rule of Thumb

- Describing an **action in progress** → **gerade**
- Marking a **change or a decision** → **jetzt**

If you could add "at the moment" in English, use *gerade*. If you could add "as of now," use *jetzt*.

They also combine: **gerade jetzt** = right at this very moment, emphatic — often with a note of bad timing: *Warum jetzt? Ausgerechnet gerade jetzt!*

## Common Mistakes

- **Building an "-ing" tense.** German has no *Ich bin arbeiten*. Use the plain present plus *gerade*: **Ich arbeite gerade.**
- **Using *jetzt* to decline a call.** *Ich arbeite jetzt* sounds like a life update. *Ich arbeite gerade* is the one that means "call me back."
- **Overusing *gerade jetzt*.** It's emphatic; most sentences want one word or the other, not both.
