## Self-Check


1. Choose the natural word: *Das Hotel ist ___ teuer.*\
   a. sehr\
   b. viel

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *sehr teuer* (adjective).

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Wir haben ___ Arbeit.*\
   a. sehr\
   b. viel

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *viel Arbeit* (noun).

</details>

3. Is **sehr viel Geld** correct?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Yes

**Explanation:** "a very great deal of money." *sehr* intensifies *viel*, which counts the money.

</details>

4. Why is *Ich habe sehr Zeit* wrong?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *Zeit* is a noun, and nouns take *viel*: **Ich habe viel Zeit.**

**Explanation:** *sehr* expresses intensity or degree; *viel* expresses quantity or amount.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "he speaks very quickly" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Er spricht sehr schnell.**

**Explanation:** *sehr* expresses intensity or degree; *viel* expresses quantity or amount.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "I don't have much time"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich habe nicht viel Zeit.**

**Explanation:** *sehr* expresses intensity or degree; *viel* expresses quantity or amount.

</details>
