# Quiz: bei vs. mit

## Self-Check


1. You're staying at your parents' house. Which is right?\
   a. Ich wohne mit meinen Eltern zusammen.\
   b. Ich wohne bei meinen Eltern.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *bei meinen Eltern* (it's their home). *mit … zusammen* would suggest an equal flatshare.

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich arbeite ___ einer Bank.*\
   a. mit\
   b. bei

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *bei einer Bank.*

</details>

3. Choose the natural word: *Ich fahre ___ dem Bus.*\
   a. mit\
   b. bei

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *mit dem Bus.*

</details>

4. Which preposition would you use for *going* to someone's place?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **zu**

**Explanation:** *Ich gehe zu meinem Freund.* Neither *bei* nor *mit* works for movement.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Say you're at your friend's place right now.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich bin bei meinem Freund.**

**Explanation:** Or *bei meiner Freundin* for a female friend.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** Say you're travelling with your wife.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Ich reise mit meiner Frau.** (*Ich fahre mit meiner Frau* also works.)

**Explanation:** *bei* means being at/with someone in the sense of location or situation; *mit* means together with someone or using something.

</details>
