## Self-Check


1. Which is correct?\
   a. Ich bleibe hier, weil ich bin müde.\
   b. Ich bleibe hier, weil ich müde bin.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *weil ich müde bin* (verb at the end).

</details>

2. Which is correct?\
   a. Ich bleibe hier, denn ich bin müde.\
   b. Ich bleibe hier, denn ich müde bin.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *denn ich bin müde* (normal word order).

</details>

3. Someone asks *Warum lernst du Deutsch?* Can you start your answer with *denn*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** No. *denn* can't begin a sentence; use *weil*: **Weil ich in Deutschland arbeite.**

**Explanation:** *denn* connects two main clauses with normal word order; *weil* introduces a subordinate clause and sends the conjugated verb to the end.

</details>

4. What happens to the verb after *weil*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** It moves to the end of the clause.

**Explanation:** *denn* connects two main clauses with normal word order; *weil* introduces a subordinate clause and sends the conjugated verb to the end.

</details>
