# aus vs. von

**Compares:** [aus](../prepositions/aus.md) · [von](../prepositions/von.md)
**Level:** A1 · **Tier 1**

## The Simple Explanation

English "from" covers both. German splits them:

> **aus** — **out of the inside of something.** Your home country, a building, a container. Where you're *from* in a lasting sense.
> **von** — a **starting point or a source.** Where this particular journey began, or who gave you something.

Rough test: *aus* is where you belong; *von* is where you've just come from.

## Contrasting Examples

**Ich komme aus Amerika.**
I'm from America. *(my origin)*

**Ich komme von der Arbeit.**
I'm coming from work. *(today's starting point)*

---

**Sie kommt aus Berlin.**
She's from Berlin. *(she grew up there — with a person and a city, this is nearly always the one you want)*

**Sie fährt von Berlin nach Hamburg.**
She's travelling from Berlin to Hamburg. *(here Berlin is only the start of a journey, so von — and note it needs the nach half to sound natural)*

---

**Das Geschenk ist von meiner Mutter.**
The present is from my mother. *(a person → von)*

**Der Tisch ist aus Holz.**
The table is made of wood. *(material → aus)*

---

**Er steigt aus dem Bus.**
He's getting off the bus. *(out of the inside)*

**Der Bus kommt vom Bahnhof.**
The bus comes from the station. *(starting point)*

## Rule of Thumb

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Your country, city, or region of origin | **aus** |
| Coming out of a building or vehicle | **aus** |
| Made of a material | **aus** |
| Today's starting point (work, the station, home) | **von** |
| A person who gave, sent, or said something | **von** |
| "of" — belonging | **von** |
| from … to … (a span) | **von … bis …** |

**The one to fix first:** nationality and hometown always take **aus**. *Ich komme aus …* is the standard answer to *Woher kommst du?*

## Common Mistakes

- ***Ich komme von Kanada.*** → **aus Kanada.** Probably the most frequent preposition error at A1.
- ***ein Geschenk aus meiner Mutter.*** → **von meiner Mutter.** People are sources, not containers.
- **Using *aus* for today's journey.** *Ich komme aus der Arbeit* sounds like you emerged from inside the concept of work; say **von der Arbeit**.
- **Forgetting the contractions.** *von dem* → **vom**. (*aus dem* has no short form.)
