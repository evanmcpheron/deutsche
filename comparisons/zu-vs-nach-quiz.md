## Self-Check


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich fahre ___ Hamburg.*\
   a. zu\
   b. nach

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *nach Hamburg* (a city).

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich gehe ___ Zahnarzt.*\
   a. zum\
   b. nach

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *zum Zahnarzt* (a person).

</details>

3. Choose the natural word: *Wir fliegen ___ Spanien.*\
   a. zu\
   b. nach

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *nach Spanien* (a country with no article).

</details>

4. Which destination type always takes *zu*, without exception?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** People. *zu meiner Mutter*, *zum Arzt*, *zu dir*.

**Explanation:** *nach* is used for cities, most countries without articles, and directions; *zu* is common with people and many specific destinations.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm going to my brother's" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich gehe zu meinem Bruder.**

**Explanation:** *Ich fahre …* if it's a drive.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "how do I get to the station?"

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Wie komme ich zum Bahnhof?**

**Explanation:** *nach* is used for cities, most countries without articles, and directions; *zu* is common with people and many specific destinations.

</details>
