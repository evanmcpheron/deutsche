## Self-Check


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich komme ___ Irland.* (that's where I'm from)\
   a. aus\
   b. von

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *aus Irland.*

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich komme gerade ___ Bahnhof.*\
   a. aus dem\
   b. vom

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *vom Bahnhof* (starting point of this trip).

</details>

3. Choose the natural word: *Das ist ein Brief ___ meinem Vater.*\
   a. aus\
   b. von

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *von meinem Vater* (a person).

</details>

4. Choose the natural word: *Die Tasche ist ___ Leder.* (made of leather)\
   a. aus\
   b. von

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *aus Leder* (material).

</details>

5. **Produce it:** Answer the question *Woher kommen Sie?* — say you're from Germany.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich komme aus Deutschland.**

**Explanation:** *aus* usually marks origin from inside a place or a country; *von* often marks a source or starting point such as work or another person.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** Say you're coming from work.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich komme von der Arbeit.**

**Explanation:** *aus* usually marks origin from inside a place or a country; *von* often marks a source or starting point such as work or another person.

</details>
