1. `ich + sein` in Präteritum → **_____**

</details>

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

## Part C — Recognize Modal Past Forms

10. `konnte` comes from:
   a. kommen
   b. können
   c. kennen
   d. kaufen

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b — können**

</details>

11. **Ich musste arbeiten.** means:
   a. I could work.
   b. I wanted to work.
   c. I had to work.
   d. I worked yesterday.

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **c**

</details>

12. **Er wollte nach Hause gehen.** means:

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **He wanted to go home.**

</details>

13. Complete with the most natural form:

```text
Ich ___ nicht kommen. (können, past)
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich konnte nicht kommen.**

Recognition is the main A1 target, but this common form is useful to produce.

</details>

14. Which should generally be the higher A1 production priority for an ordinary completed action such as “I learned German”?
   a. full Präteritum (`ich lernte`)
   b. Perfekt (`ich habe gelernt`)

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b — Perfekt**

</details>
