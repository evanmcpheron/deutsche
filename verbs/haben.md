# haben — to have

**Type:** Irregular verb
**Level:** A1
**Priority:** Core — essential
**Knowledge goal:** Active production

[← `sein`](sein.md) | [Verb Index](README.md) | [Next → `werden`](werden.md) | [Irregular Verb Quiz →](essential-irregular-verbs-quiz.md)

## Core Idea

`haben` is mostly regular-looking, but the `du` and `er/sie/es` forms lose the `b`.

| Person | Present |
|---|---|
| ich | **habe** |
| du | **hast** |
| er / sie / es | **hat** |
| wir | **haben** |
| ihr | **habt** |
| sie / Sie | **haben** |

## High-Frequency Uses

### Possession

**Ich habe ein Auto.**
I have a car.

**Hast du Zeit?**
Do you have time?

### Common Expressions

**Ich habe Hunger.** — I am hungry.
**Ich habe Durst.** — I am thirsty.
**Ich habe Angst.** — I am afraid.
**Ich habe keine Zeit.** — I do not have time.

German often uses `haben + noun` where English uses “be + adjective.”

### As a Perfekt Auxiliary

Later, `haben` also helps build the conversational past:

**Ich habe Deutsch gelernt.**
I learned / have learned German.

The auxiliary is conjugated; the past participle goes later in the clause. See [Perfekt](perfekt.md).

## Word Order

**Heute habe ich keine Zeit.**

**Hast du Hunger?**

**Warum habt ihr so wenig Zeit?**

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- ❌ `du habst` → ✅ **du hast**
- ❌ `er habt` → ✅ **er hat**
- ❌ `Ich bin Hunger.` → ✅ **Ich habe Hunger.**
- Forgetting that Perfekt uses a **conjugated** auxiliary: `ich habe`, `du hast`, `wir haben`.

## Production Target

Know all six present forms and the common chunks `Hunger haben`, `Durst haben`, `Zeit haben`, and `Angst haben`.
