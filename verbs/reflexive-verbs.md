# Reflexive Verbs

**Type:** Verb + pronoun pattern
**Level:** A1
**Priority:** Core for common phrases
**Knowledge goal:** Active with familiar verbs

## Core Idea

A reflexive verb uses a pronoun that refers back to the subject.

**Ich wasche mich.**
I wash myself.

**Du fühlst dich gut.**
You feel well.

At A1, focus on the common accusative reflexive pronouns.

| Subject | Reflexive pronoun |
|---|---|
| ich | **mich** |
| du | **dich** |
| er / sie / es | **sich** |
| wir | **uns** |
| ihr | **euch** |
| sie / Sie | **sich** |

The verb itself still conjugates normally.

## Useful A1 Patterns

### `sich fühlen` — to feel

**Ich fühle mich gut.**
I feel well.

**Wie fühlst du dich?**
How do you feel?

### `sich waschen` — to wash oneself

**Ich wasche mich.**

### `sich setzen` — to sit down

**Setzen Sie sich, bitte.**
Please sit down.

### `sich vorstellen` — to introduce oneself

**Ich stelle mich vor.**
I introduce myself.

This verb is also separable: `vorstellen` → **stelle ... vor**. The reflexive pronoun stays inside the clause.

## Word Order

**Ich fühle mich heute gut.**

**Heute fühle ich mich gut.**

**Wie fühlst du dich?**

The conjugated verb follows normal word-order rules. The reflexive pronoun does not replace the subject.

## Not Every English Reflexive Idea Matches German

Do not add `sich` simply because English can say “myself.” Learn reflexive verbs as actual German patterns.

Likewise, some German reflexive verbs translate naturally without “myself” in English:

**Ich fühle mich gut.**
I feel good.

## A Note on Dative Reflexives

German also has dative reflexive forms such as **mir** and **dir** in patterns like `Ich wasche mir die Hände`. That distinction is useful, but it should not compete with the basic A1 accusative pattern yet. Recognize it when you meet it; master the table above first.

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- ❌ `Ich fühle ich gut.` → ✅ **Ich fühle mich gut.**
- ❌ `Du fühlst sich gut.` → ✅ **Du fühlst dich gut.**
- ❌ `Wir fühlen sich gut.` → ✅ **Wir fühlen uns gut.**
- Forgetting that a verb can be both separable and reflexive: **Ich stelle mich vor.**
