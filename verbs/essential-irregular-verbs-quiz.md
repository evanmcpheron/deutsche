# Quiz: `sein`, `haben`, and `werden`

[← `sein`](sein.md) | [`haben`](haben.md) | [`werden`](werden.md) | [Verb Index](README.md)

These three verbs are frequent enough that the forms should become direct retrieval, not rule calculation.

---

## Part A — Produce the Form

1. `ich + sein` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **ich bin**

</details>

2. `ihr + sein` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **ihr seid**

</details>

3. `du + haben` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **du hast**

</details>

4. `er + haben` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **er hat**

</details>

5. `du + werden` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **du wirst**

</details>

6. `es + werden` → **_____**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **es wird**

</details>

---

## Part B — Choose by Meaning

7. You are tired now.

```text
Ich ___ müde.
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **bin**

A state uses `sein`.

</details>

8. You are starting to feel tired.

```text
Ich ___ müde.
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **werde**

A change into a state uses `werden`.

</details>

9. Complete naturally: “I am hungry.”

```text
Ich ___ Hunger.
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **habe**

German says `Hunger haben`.

</details>

10. Complete the question: “Where are you?”

```text
Wo ___ du?
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **bist**

</details>

---

## Part C — Sentence Production

11. Put the words in order.

```text
heute / ich / zu Hause / bin
```

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Heute bin ich zu Hause.**

The conjugated verb is second.

</details>

12. Translate: **Do you have time?**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Hast du Zeit?**

</details>

13. Translate: **It is getting cold.**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Es wird kalt.**

</details>

14. Correct the sentence:

**Ihr sind müde.**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ihr seid müde.**

</details>

15. Correct the sentence:

**Du werdst 36.**

<details><summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Du wirst 36.**

</details>
