# sein — to be

**Type:** Irregular verb
**Level:** A1
**Priority:** Core — essential
**Knowledge goal:** Active production

## Core Idea

`sein` is one of the most important German verbs and is fully irregular. Do not try to derive these forms from normal endings; learn them as complete forms.

| Person | Present |
|---|---|
| ich | **bin** |
| du | **bist** |
| er / sie / es | **ist** |
| wir | **sind** |
| ihr | **seid** |
| sie / Sie | **sind** |

## High-Frequency Uses

### Identity

**Ich bin Evan.**
I am Evan.

### Description

**Das ist gut.**
That is good.

**Wir sind müde.**
We are tired.

### Location

**Ich bin zu Hause.**
I am at home.

**Wo bist du?**
Where are you?

### Age

German uses `sein`, not `haben`, for age.

**Ich bin 35 Jahre alt.**
I am 35 years old.

### With `da`

**Ich bin wieder da.**
I am back / here again.

## Word Order

`sein` obeys the same verb-second rule as other conjugated verbs.

**Heute bin ich zu Hause.**

**Bist du müde?**

**Warum seid ihr hier?**

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- ❌ `ich sein` → ✅ **ich bin**
- ❌ `du sind` → ✅ **du bist**
- ❌ `ihr sind` → ✅ **ihr seid**
- ❌ `Ich habe 35 Jahre.` → ✅ **Ich bin 35 Jahre alt.**
- Confusing **seid** (you all are) with **seit** (since).

## Past Forms You Will Meet Later

The common Präteritum forms begin:

**ich war, du warst, er war, wir waren, ihr wart, sie waren**

Do not mix them into present-tense practice yet. See [Essential Präteritum](praeteritum-essentials.md).

## Production Target

You should be able to produce all six present forms without calculating them.
