# Verb Conjugation

[← Repository README](../README.md) | [Start Here](../START-HERE.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

This section teaches the **A1 verb system you need to understand and produce ordinary German sentences**. The goal is not to memorize giant tables. The goal is to connect:

> **subject → verb form → sentence pattern → meaning**

Verb conjugation is production-heavy. If a lesson asks you to write **du fährst**, then *du fahrst* is not an alternative spelling: the umlaut and ending are part of the form.

## What You Are Expected to Produce at A1

Work toward reliable production of:

- regular present-tense endings for all six grammatical persons;
- `sein` and `haben` without needing to derive them;
- common stem-changing present forms, especially `du` and `er/sie/es`;
- common modal verbs with an infinitive at the end;
- common separable verbs with the prefix in the correct position;
- basic reflexive pronouns with familiar verbs;
- conversational past statements using **Perfekt**;
- the very common Präteritum forms **war** and **hatte**, plus recognition of common modal past forms.

You do **not** need to memorize every irregular German verb or produce literary Präteritum across the whole verb system.

## The Six Person Forms

| Person | English idea |
|---|---|
| `ich` | I |
| `du` | you, singular informal |
| `er / sie / es` | he / she / it |
| `wir` | we |
| `ihr` | you, plural informal |
| `sie / Sie` | they / you, formal |

`Sie` uses the same verb form as `sie` (they), but the capital letter matters.

## How the Quizzes Work

The quizzes deliberately mix recognition and production, but production carries more weight here than it does in the small-word sections.

- **Recognition:** choose or interpret the correct form.
- **Controlled production:** given a subject and infinitive, write the conjugated form.
- **Sentence production:** complete or build a short sentence with correct word order.
- **Mixed retrieval:** identify the needed pattern without the page title telling you which rule applies.

Every answer is hidden in a `<details>` solution directly below the question.

## Accuracy Standard

For production questions, count the answer correct only when the required verb form is correctly spelled. That includes:

- endings: `machst`, not `mach`;
- umlauts: `fährst`, not `fahrst`;
- stem changes: `gibst`, not `gebst`;
- inserted `e`: `arbeitest`, not `arbeitst`;
- separated prefixes where required: `Ich stehe auf`, not `Ich aufstehe`;
- the correct auxiliary and participle in Perfekt: `Ich bin gegangen`, not `Ich habe gegangen`.

Minor punctuation outside the target form does not need to turn a correct conjugation answer into a failure.

## Important Principle

A conjugation table is a reference, not the end goal. Always connect a form to a sentence:

> `du fährst` → **Du fährst heute nach Berlin.**

> `er steht auf` → **Er steht um sieben Uhr auf.**

> `wir haben gemacht` → **Wir haben das gestern gemacht.**

That is the level at which the verb becomes usable.
