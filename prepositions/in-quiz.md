## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural form: *Ich gehe ___ Kino.*\
   a. im\
   b. ins\
   c. in der

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *ins Kino* (movement → accusative).

</details>

2. Choose the natural form: *Das Buch ist ___ Regal.*\
   a. im\
   b. ins\
   c. in das

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** *im Regal* (location → dative).

</details>

3. Which question does **Wir fahren in die Stadt** answer?\
   a. Wo?\
   b. Wohin?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Wohin?* Movement, so accusative: *in die Stadt.*

</details>

4. Contrast: **Ich bin in der Küche.** / **Ich gehe in die Küche.** What's different, and which word carries the difference?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** The first says where I am; the second says where I'm heading. The difference is carried by the article: *der Küche* (dative) vs. *die Küche* (accusative).

**Explanation:** *in* is a two-way preposition: location answers *Wo?* with dative, while movement toward a destination answers *Wohin?* with accusative.

</details>
