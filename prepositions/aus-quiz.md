## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich komme ___ Kanada.*\
   a. von\
   b. aus\
   c. bei

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich komme aus Kanada.*

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich komme gerade ___ der Arbeit.* (just coming from work)\
   a. aus\
   b. von\
   c. nach

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *von der Arbeit.* Work is a starting point for today's journey, not your origin.

</details>

3. **Der Film ist aus.** What does this mean?\
   a. The film is out (released).\
   b. The film is over.\
   c. The film is from abroad.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** the film is over.

</details>

4. Contrast: **Ich komme aus Berlin.** / **Ich fahre von Berlin nach Hamburg.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *aus Berlin* = Berlin is where I'm from. *von Berlin nach Hamburg* = Berlin is only where this journey starts

**Explanation:** that's the one situation where a city takes *von*, and it needs the *nach* half to sound natural. For a city of origin, always *aus*.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm from America" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich komme aus Amerika.**

**Explanation:** Or *aus den USA*.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "the table is made of wood"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Der Tisch ist aus Holz.**

**Explanation:** *aus* takes the dative and commonly expresses origin from inside a place or material something is made from.

</details>
