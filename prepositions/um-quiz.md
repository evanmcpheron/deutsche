## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Der Zug fährt ___ neun Uhr.*\
   a. in\
   b. um\
   c. am

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *um neun Uhr.*

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Wir treffen uns ___ Montag.*\
   a. um\
   b. am\
   c. in

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *am Montag.*

</details>

3. **In Köln bitte umsteigen.** What should you do?\
   a. Get off for good.\
   b. Change trains.\
   c. Stay on board.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** change trains.

</details>

4. Which preposition goes with clock times, and which with days?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Clock times take *um* (*um acht Uhr*); days take *am* (*am Montag*).

**Explanation:** *um* always takes the accusative and is used for clock times and the sense of “around.”

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "the film starts at eight" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Der Film beginnt um acht.**

**Explanation:** *um* always takes the accusative and is used for clock times and the sense of “around.”

</details>

6. **Produce it:** Ask what time someone is coming.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** A natural way to say this is **Um wie viel Uhr kommst du?**

**Explanation:** *um* always takes the accusative and is used for clock times and the sense of “around.”

</details>
