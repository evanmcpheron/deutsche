## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Ich gehe ___ Arzt.*\
   a. nach\
   b. zum\
   c. in

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *Ich gehe zum Arzt.* People and professionals always take *zu*.

</details>

2. What does **Das ist zu teuer** mean?\
   a. That's expensive to buy.\
   b. That's too expensive.\
   c. That's the expensive one.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** too expensive.

</details>

3. Fill in the pattern: *Wie komme ich ___ Bahnhof?*

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **zum**

**Explanation:** *Wie komme ich zum Bahnhof?*

</details>

4. Contrast: **Ich bin zu Hause.** / **Ich gehe nach Hause.**

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *zu Hause* describes where you are: at home. *nach Hause* describes movement: going home.

**Explanation:** Prepositional *zu* takes the dative and is common with people and many destinations; adverbial *zu* means “too” before adjectives or adverbs.

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm going to my brother's" in German, using *zu*?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich gehe zu meinem Bruder.**

**Explanation:** Or *Ich fahre zu meinem Bruder*, if it's far enough to drive.

</details>

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "the coffee is too hot"?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Der Kaffee ist zu heiß.**

**Explanation:** Prepositional *zu* takes the dative and is common with people and many destinations; adverbial *zu* means “too” before adjectives or adverbs.

</details>
