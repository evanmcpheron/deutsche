# bei

**Type:** Preposition (**always dative**)
**Level:** A1
**Priority:** Tier 1 — high
**Knowledge goal:** Active

[← nach](nach.md) | [Category Index](README.md) | [Next → aus](aus.md) | [↑ Master Index](../MASTER_INDEX.md)

## Core Idea

*bei* places you **in someone's orbit** — at their home, at their workplace, in their vicinity. It's a *location* word, never a movement word.

If you're **going** to someone, that's [zu](zu.md). Once you're **there**, it's *bei*.

> Ich gehe **zu** meiner Schwester. — I'm going to my sister's.
> Ich bin **bei** meiner Schwester. — I'm at my sister's.

**Case: always dative.** Contraction: *bei dem* → **beim**.

## Most Important A1 Uses

### 1. At someone's home or place

**Ich bin bei meiner Mutter.**
I'm at my mother's.

**Wir essen heute bei Anna.**
We're eating at Anna's today.

**Kommst du zu mir oder treffen wir uns bei dir?**
Are you coming to mine, or shall we meet at yours?

### 2. At a business or institution — where someone works

**Ich arbeite bei Siemens.**
I work at Siemens.

**Er ist beim Arzt.**
He's at the doctor's.

**Ich war beim Friseur.**
I was at the hairdresser's.

Note the pattern: *zum Arzt* = going there, *beim Arzt* = being there.

### 3. Near, by

**Das Hotel ist beim Bahnhof.**
The hotel is near the station.

**Wir treffen uns beim Bäcker.**
We'll meet at the bakery.

### 4. "While, during" — with an activity

**beim Essen**
while eating / at dinner

**Beim Frühstück lese ich Zeitung.**
I read the paper at breakfast.

**Vorsicht beim Aussteigen!**
Careful when getting off! *(announcements on trains)*

## Common Chunks

- **bei mir / bei dir / bei uns** — at my/your/our place
- **beim Arzt, beim Friseur, beim Bäcker** — at the doctor's/hairdresser's/baker's
- **bei der Arbeit** — at work
- **beim Essen** — while eating
- **Bei uns ist das anders.** — Where we're from, it's different.

## Pattern Recognition

**Ich wohne bei meinen Eltern.**
**Er ist beim Zahnarzt.**
**Wir treffen uns bei mir.**
**Sie arbeitet bei einer Bank.**
**Beim Kochen höre ich Musik.**

All static: no one is travelling in any of these sentences.

## What Changes Without It?

**Ich bin zu Hause.** — I'm at my own home.
**Ich bin bei Anna.** — I'm at Anna's home.

And the movement/location pair:

**Ich fahre zu meiner Schwester.** — I'm on my way there.
**Ich bin bei meiner Schwester.** — I've arrived; I'm there now.

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- **Using *bei* for "with."** *Ich gehe bei meinem Freund* is wrong for "I'm going with my friend" — that's **mit meinem Freund**. See [bei vs. mit](../comparisons/bei-vs-mit.md).
- **Using *bei* for movement.** *Ich gehe bei meiner Mutter* → **Ich gehe zu meiner Mutter.**
- **Forgetting *beim*.** *bei dem Arzt* is grammatical but stilted; say **beim Arzt**.
- **Translating "by" literally.** English "by the station" is *beim Bahnhof*, but "by train" is *mit dem Zug* and "written by" is *von*. English "by" scatters across several German prepositions.

## Active or Passive?

**Active** for uses 1–3. *bei mir*, *beim Arzt*, and *Ich arbeite bei …* come up constantly. Use 4 (*beim Essen*) is worth recognizing and easy to pick up later.

## You May Hear

- **Bei mir oder bei dir?** — Your place or mine?
- **Er ist gerade beim Essen.** — He's eating right now.
- **Ich arbeite bei einer kleinen Firma.** — I work at a small company.
- **Bei Regen bleiben wir zu Hause.** — If it rains, we're staying home.

   a. zu
   b. bei
   c. nach

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich gehe ___ meiner Mutter.* (I'm going to my mother's)
   a. zu
   b. bei
   c. nach

3. **Er ist beim Arzt.** What's the situation?
   a. He's on his way to the doctor.
   b. He's at the doctor's now.
   c. He's a doctor.

4. What's wrong with **Ich gehe bei meinem Freund** if you mean "I'm going with my friend"?

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I work at a bank" in German, using *bei*?

6. **Produce it:** How would you say "we're meeting at my place"?
