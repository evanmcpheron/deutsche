# um

**Priority:** Tier 1 — high
**Knowledge goal:** Active

## Core Idea

Two jobs, and at A1 the first one is the important one:

1. **Clock times** — *um acht Uhr* (at eight o'clock)
2. **Around something** — *um den Tisch* (around the table)

They connect if you picture a clock face: a point you circle round to. But you don't need the connection — just learn *um* + clock time as a chunk.

**Case: always accusative.**

## Most Important A1 Uses

### 1. Clock times — "at"

**Der Zug fährt um acht Uhr.**
The train leaves at eight o'clock.

**Wir treffen uns um halb sieben.**
We're meeting at half past six.

**Um wie viel Uhr?**
At what time?

This is the single most useful thing on this page. English says "at eight"; German says *um acht*.

### 2. Around — physically

**Wir sitzen um den Tisch.**
We're sitting around the table.

**Der Bus fährt um die Ecke.**
The bus is going around the corner.

### 3. Approximately *(with **herum** or on its own)*

**Ich komme so um acht.**
I'll come around eight.

**um die 20 Euro**
around 20 euros

### 4. As a separable prefix — *umsteigen*

**In Köln müssen Sie umsteigen.**
You have to change trains in Cologne.

Worth knowing before any train journey in Germany.

## Common Chunks

- **um acht Uhr / um halb neun / um Viertel nach drei** — at eight / half past eight / quarter past three
- **Um wie viel Uhr …?** — At what time …?
- **um die Ecke** — around the corner
- **um … herum** — around (emphatic)
- **umsteigen** — to change (trains, buses)

## Pattern Recognition

**Der Film beginnt um acht.**
**Ich stehe um sechs Uhr auf.**
**Das Geschäft öffnet um neun.**
**Wir essen um eins.**
**Um wie viel Uhr kommst du?**

If a clock time is involved, the preposition is almost always *um*.

## What Changes Without It?

**Ich komme acht Uhr.** ✗
**Ich komme um acht Uhr.** ✓

German doesn't drop the preposition before clock times the way English sometimes does ("see you eight-ish"). But note that with days and parts of the day, German switches to *am*: **am Montag**, **am Abend** — not *um*.

| Time expression | Preposition |
|---|---|
| clock time | **um** acht Uhr |
| day | **am** Montag |
| part of day | **am** Abend, **am** Morgen |
| month, season | **im** Juli, **im** Sommer |
| year | *(nothing)* 2026, or **im Jahr** 2026 |

## Common English-Speaker Mistakes

- **Using *in* or *an* for clock times.** *in acht Uhr* → **um acht Uhr**.
- **Using *um* for days.** *um Montag* → **am Montag**.
- **Forgetting *umsteigen*.** If an announcement says *bitte umsteigen*, you need to change trains — not get off for good.

## Active or Passive?

**Active** for clock times, which you'll need constantly for appointments, trains, and opening hours. **Recognition First** for the "around" uses.

## You May Hear

- **Um wie viel Uhr?** — At what time?
- **Wir haben ab acht Uhr geöffnet.** — We're open from eight.
- **In Frankfurt bitte umsteigen.** — Please change trains in Frankfurt.
- **So um zehn, ja?** — Around ten, then?
