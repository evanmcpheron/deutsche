## Mini Review


1. Choose the natural word: *Das ist ein Geschenk ___ meiner Mutter.*\
   a. aus\
   b. von\
   c. bei

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *von meiner Mutter.*

</details>

2. Choose the natural word: *Ich komme ___ Spanien.* (that's my home country)\
   a. von\
   b. aus\
   c. bei

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** *aus Spanien.* Origin takes *aus*.

</details>

3. **Wir haben von Montag bis Freitag geöffnet.** What's being described?\
   a. Opening hours across the week.\
   b. A holiday closure.\
   c. A single day.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** the days they're open.

</details>

4. How would a German speaker normally say "my sister's car" in everyday speech?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **das Auto von meiner Schwester.** The genitive (*das Auto meiner Schwester*) is correct but more formal than you need at A1.

**Explanation:** *von* takes the dative and commonly marks a source, starting point, or relationship similar to “of/from.”

</details>

5. **Produce it:** How would you say "I'm coming from work" in German?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **Ich komme von der Arbeit.**

**Explanation:** *von* takes the dative and commonly marks a source, starting point, or relationship similar to “of/from.”

</details>
