Answer all questions before scrolling to the key. Most of these ask what **situation** the German describes — you don't need to translate anything.

---

## Part A — Interpret the Sentence

**1.** Ich bin noch nicht fertig.
- a. I finished earlier.
- b. I have not finished yet.
- c. I am starting tomorrow.

**2.** Er ist schon hier.
- a. He hasn't arrived.
- b. He arrived, possibly earlier than expected.
- c. He's leaving.

**3.** Ich habe keine Zeit.
- a. I have some time.
- b. I have no time.
- c. I'll have time later.

**4.** Wir haben nur noch zwei Karten.
- a. Two tickets are left, and that's all.
- b. Two tickets have been sold.
- c. Tickets cost two euros.

**5.** Ich auch nicht.
- a. Me too.
- b. Me neither.
- c. Not me.

**6.** Das Restaurant ist noch offen.
- a. It hasn't closed yet.
- b. It has reopened.
- c. It's closed.

---

## Part B — Choose the Natural Word

**7.** Ich bin ___ nicht fertig.\
   a. schon\
   b. noch\
   c. aus

**8.** Ich habe ___ Geld.\
   a. nicht\
   b. kein\
   c. keine

**9.** Das Wetter ist ___ schön.\
   a. nicht\
   b. kein\
   c. nur

**10.** Wir fahren ___ nach München. *(we're going too)*\
   a. auch\
   b. noch\
   c. erst

**11.** Es ist ___ acht Uhr — wir müssen los! *(already eight)*\
   a. noch\
   b. schon\
   c. nur

**12.** Das kostet ___ fünf Euro. *(only five)*\
   a. nur\
   b. noch\
   c. auch

## Part C — What's the Difference?

**13.** Ich möchte einen Kaffee. / Ich möchte noch einen Kaffee.

**14.** Ich bin hier. / Ich bin noch hier.

**15.** Ich habe eine Frage. / Ich habe nur eine Frage.

---

## Part D — Produce It

Write the German yourself. More than one answer can be right.

**16.** How would you say "I'm not finished yet", using *noch*?

**17.** How would you say "I don't have any time", using *kein*?

**18.** Someone says they're tired. How would you say "me too"?

**19.** How would you say "that's too expensive"?

**20.** In a café, ask for another coffee.

---
---

## Answers

**Part A**
1. **b** — not finished yet.
2. **b** — already arrived.
3. **b** — no time.
4. **a** — only two left. *nur noch* = only … left.
5. **b** — me neither.
6. **a** — it hasn't closed yet. *(Reopening after closing would be **wieder offen**.)*

**Part B**
7. **b** — *Ich bin noch nicht fertig.*
8. **b** — *Ich habe kein Geld.* (*Geld* is neuter → no ending.)
9. **a** — *Das Wetter ist nicht schön.* (Adjective → *nicht*.)
10. **a** — *Wir fahren auch nach München.*
11. **b** — *Es ist schon acht Uhr.*
12. **a** — *Das kostet nur fünf Euro.*

**Part C**
13. Without *noch*, you're ordering your first coffee. With *noch*, you've already had one and want another.
14. *Ich bin hier* is a plain fact. *Ich bin noch hier* says you haven't left yet — and implies someone might have expected you to.
15. *nur* shrinks the request: "I've just got one question, this won't take long." It's noticeably politer.

**Part D**
16. **Ich bin noch nicht fertig.**
17. **Ich habe keine Zeit.**
18. **Ich auch.**
19. **Das ist zu teuer.**
20. A natural way to say this is **Noch einen Kaffee, bitte.**

### How Did It Go?

- **16+ correct:** move on to Stage 2 in [Start Here](../START_HERE.md).
- **10–15:** reread the two or three word pages that gave you trouble, then retake this in a day or two.
- **Under 10:** go back through Stage 1 slowly. Nothing is wrong — these six words carry a lot, and they take repetition.
