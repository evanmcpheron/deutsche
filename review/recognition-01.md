## Part A — Interpret the Sentence

**1.** Ich bin noch nicht fertig.
- a. I finished earlier.
- b. I have not finished yet.
- c. I am starting tomorrow.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** not finished yet.

</details>

**2.** Er ist schon hier.
- a. He hasn't arrived.
- b. He arrived, possibly earlier than expected.
- c. He's leaving.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** already arrived.

</details>

**3.** Ich habe keine Zeit.
- a. I have some time.
- b. I have no time.
- c. I'll have time later.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** no time.

</details>

**4.** Wir haben nur noch zwei Karten.
- a. Two tickets are left, and that's all.
- b. Two tickets have been sold.
- c. Tickets cost two euros.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** only two left. *nur noch* = only … left.

</details>

**5.** Ich auch nicht.
- a. Me too.
- b. Me neither.
- c. Not me.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **b**

**Explanation:** me neither.

</details>

**6.** Das Restaurant ist noch offen.
- a. It hasn't closed yet.
- b. It has reopened.
- c. It's closed.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** **a**

**Explanation:** it hasn't closed yet. *(Reopening after closing would be **wieder offen**.)*

</details>

**7.** Ich bin ___ nicht fertig.\
   a. schon\
   b. noch\

**Answer:** **b**

**8.** Ich habe ___ Geld.\
   a. nicht\
   b. kein\
   c. keine

**9.** Das Wetter ist ___ schön.\
   a. nicht\

</details>

## Part C — What's the Difference?

**13.** Ich möchte einen Kaffee. / Ich möchte noch einen Kaffee.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** Without *noch*, you're ordering your first coffee. With *noch*, you've already had one and want another.

**Explanation:** This matches the Stage 1 meaning or sentence pattern being tested; focus on the situation described rather than a word-for-word translation.

</details>

**14.** Ich bin hier. / Ich bin noch hier.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *Ich bin hier* is a plain fact. *Ich bin noch hier* says you haven't left yet

**Explanation:** and implies someone might have expected you to.

</details>

**15.** Ich habe eine Frage. / Ich habe nur eine Frage.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

**Answer:** *nur* shrinks the request: "I've just got one question, this won't take long." It's noticeably politer.

**Explanation:** This matches the Stage 1 meaning or sentence pattern being tested; focus on the situation described rather than a word-for-word translation.

</details>
